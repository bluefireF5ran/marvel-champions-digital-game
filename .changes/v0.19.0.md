### Engine
* Vulnerable: a character that becomes stunned or confused is discarded without being defeated, ahead of simultaneous damage (RRG 1.8 p. 48)
* All-purpose counters: a counter lands as the type its card defines, removal and counts can take any type, moveCounters moves a chosen amount (MC50 p. 3)
* Threat on cards that are not schemes follows no scheme rules, and removing threat can be an ability cost with a chosen amount
* Enemy scheme threat can be diverted to another card, and card picks can be capped by total printed cost
* Preparation abilities (Black Widow) as their own trigger kind, and look-at over dealt encounter cards with rearranging
* Play from the deck at a reduced cost, an in-play cost that counts the card paying it, and damage reductions that except an attack by card or keyword (wave 9)
* A rule can give a labeled ability (a Preparation) to every encounter card that prints none (wave 9 engine task 10)
* A Preparation can prevent all damage from a player's attack and report how much; an attack moved onto a guard minion now deals its damage (wave 9 engine task 11)
* An interrupt on a card face turned up by a forced interrupt in the same window is offered (Nick Fury's Assault after Break Cover); a resource ability can generate one resource per threat removed as its cost (Fury's Watch)
* An effect can repeat a counted number of times (All-Points Bulletin); a defeated card is matched by what it was when defeated, so a facedown Controlled minion's defeat is recognized
* An effect can resolve once per card of a set with that card bound (Security Cameras); a cost can discard cards tucked under a card (Cindy Moon)
* A reveal in progress can be replaced by the reveal of another card (Eidetic Memory)
* A card leaving play records whether an encounter card or a player card effect caused it (Front Organization); an event after encounter cards are dealt (Intelligence)
* A resource card's own Interrupt can add resources to the payment that spends it (Organizational Support)
* A tuck can be heard and redirected to another host, and an ability can answer its own card being discarded from under a card (wave 9 engine tasks 23 and 24)
* A card leaving play can be tucked under another card instead (wave 9 engine task 12); a card attached from outside play now enters play, with its Uses counters
* A surge deals its card (Intelligence can answer it); an encounter card effect's discard from a hand or deck can be replaced (Front Organization)
* A scenario deck whose top card is in play: the next card enters play when the top leaves, and a card put under an empty deck enters play (wave 9 engine task 13)
* A minion can be held by an environment, engaged with nobody; engagement can rotate between players; a minion already in play that engages a player triggers quickstrike (wave 9 engine tasks 14 and 15)
* A status card about to be given can be heard and replaced; counters can be removed from among several cards, and a card picked by most or fewest counters (wave 9 engine tasks 16 and 17)
* A reset can set hit points to a printed number and still announce the reset; an ally can be attached under no player's control (a hostage), and no player may use a captive ally's Action or optional Response
* Hidden piles of cards that no view, prompt or log shows until gained or revealed (wave 9 engine task 19); a random pick from the victory display
* The encounter deck's top card can be kept faceup; a cost can discard a chosen number of cards from the top of the encounter deck (wave 9 engine tasks 25 and 26)
* A discard from the encounter deck can be heard; an ability can read what a just-resolved ability bound; a boost card being given can be answered by looking at it and the deck's top card and swapping them (wave 9 engine tasks 27 and 28)
* An encounter card about to be dealt can be heard and replaced; a resource ability can be limited per card paid for; a card's constant can grant it another ability (wave 9 engine tasks 29 and 30; Flight Squadron)
* A play knows which cards paid for it; a tucked resource card can be spent as if from hand; a character can defend without exhausting (wave 9 engine tasks 31 to 33)
* A surge deals a facedown encounter card that waits for step four (22B); a revealed minion can enter play held (26B); Down but Not Out is removed from the game either way (29B)
* A flip can keep named counters through a change of card type (Board Members, owner 1A); a card swapped into a boost position is facedown there, so it flips (Up, Up, and Away)
* Accusing the mole: accuse and identifyMole over an evidence grid, with the accused, the mole and the wrong guesses readable by later abilities
* An additional cost to attack, thwart or defend with a character (Divided Loyalties); a second Max 1 per player copy cannot be put into play (37A); the unique rule's replacement card waits facedown (38A)
* An enemy made to activate by a card offers its activation interrupts; an attack that defeats a hero records the defeat; effects can answer a scheme activation that is about to place threat (Battle of Wits)
* A hero's identity card can be in play as a minion no player controls, found in the collection by its hero title (Reluctant Foe)
* Removing fewer counters than named leaves Then unresolved; a shared title goes to the card on the ability's own side; a retargeted attack ability has the new target as its only target
* Reluctant Foe counts only faceup titles (44B); Divided Loyalties also taxes an ally's labeled ability and an effect-declared defender (48B); a named card dealt to a player can be answered (Aerial Recon with Serpent Solutions)
* A count of distinct traits; an attachment about to be attached can be redirected to another host (Fixer); a combined-stat threshold on an exhaust cost (Tech-Pac); a card that can be attacked as if it were a minion and is never defeated (Encased in Ice)
* Divided Loyalties no longer charges for an ally's labeled ability (53B); Talon Line and Aerial Recon's action work; spend X is one choice and one payment (A.I.M. Interference, Battle of Wits)
* Forced Actions must be used before the turn or phase ends; a card can be attached with its reverse side hidden; a counter about to be placed can be replaced; a character with two copies of Encased in Ice can attack either
* A cost every player pays; cannot make basic attacks; linked cards set aside for a named encounter card (Captain America's Shield is now set aside); swapping the villain by trait; a cap on counters
* The pod for God of Lies (a shared villain and scheme in a neutral area, per-group values, group choices); encounter cards ready at the end of the player phase; a linked ally can enter play while the minion that names it is being defeated
