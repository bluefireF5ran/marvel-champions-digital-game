## [v0.20.0] - 2026-10-13
### Added
* Undo beside End turn (or the Z key) takes back the hero's own last move this turn; it stops at the start of the turn, at another player's move, and at anything that drew, revealed or searched cards
* Paying for a card numbers each picked card in the order it will be discarded and tags the last one "on top", with a hover note on why the top card matters (Pepper Potts)
* Desktop and Android apps check GitHub for a newer release on launch and show an update banner on the Title screen, with the changelog and a direct download of their own installer, when it has one for their platform
### Changed
* Update Capacitor, Tauri, Playwright, Vite, Vitest, oxlint, oxfmt and lint-staged to their latest releases, and @types/node to the Node 24 line
### Fixed
* Decks & Collection: "+ New deck" sits on the Your decks header, always in view, instead of at the bottom of the deck list
* Backing out of "Play this deck" (Take your seats, then Scenarios, then Title) lands on Title instead of looping back to Take your seats
* Decks & Collection: the Duplicate button and "+ New deck" use the display face at a readable size instead of the 9px label
* A crowded play area keeps its cards at a readable size and scrolls sideways (mouse wheel, drag, or the arrows at its edges) instead of shrinking them under their own action labels (#119)
* On the defend prompt, an option's damage and hit point lines stay inside its card at every window size (#125)
* Desktop: links out of the game (release notes, MarvelCDB, rulebook PDFs) open in the default browser
