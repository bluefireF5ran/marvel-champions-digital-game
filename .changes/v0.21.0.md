## [v0.21.0] - 2026-10-14
### Added
* The Civil War box: Tigra, Hulkling, four leaders and a custom scenario builder
* Civil War games: a readable save and pause label for ready-made, custom, random and blind scenarios, and Game Over's Play again and New random scenario.
* Wonder Man is playable: his cards, scripts and nemesis set join the pool, and the deck builder offers him (his starter deck is not printed yet, so he is seated with a deck you build)
* Art for Civil War: Tigra's and Hulkling's hero pictures, the four leaders' pictures and the box cover on the pack shelf
* Hercules joins the playable game: his pack, nemesis set and All Versus All, his Leadership starter deck and Team-Up art; Wonder Man and Hercules unlock by beating Registration Iron Man and Resistance Captain America
* Guided mode: Try-it lessons for Wonder Man (overpay, tuck under Ionic Physiology, attack with the bonus) and Hercules (reveal a Labor, finish it, earn a Gift, play a card it discounts), on the Civil War page
* Community decks: four more MarvelCDB decklists (Tigra, Hulkling, Wonder Man, Hercules, each by Web-Warrior Fanatic), tested; a deck refusal now reads "an attachment card" for vowel-sound types
* Coordinated Effort on the board: a second player can help pay an ability's cost. The player using it is asked who takes each shareable part (damage, a deck discard), the helper's hand cards can be picked for a discard cost, each helper agrees in turn before their cards or hit points are used, the engine's reason is shown when a player cannot take a part, and the log says who helped.
* Game Over pictures for Iron Man and Captain Marvel as leaders, for when they win and when they lose
* Game Over pictures for Captain America and Spider-Woman as leaders, for when they win and when they lose
* In the Pause menu's game log, the moments Undo could get back to this turn are marked ↶, and tapping one rewinds there as pressing Undo that many times would.

* Hercules: the Gift count shows on his identity and on the cards that read it (with why it reads 0 under Appeal to Athena), and a refused New Labors says which part of its condition failed
* Release downloads spell out their OS, architecture and installer kind (MarvelChampions-<version>-Windows-x64-Setup.exe / -Windows-x64-AllUsers.msi, -macOS-AppleSilicon-arm64.dmg / -macOS-Intel-x64.dmg, -Linux-x64.deb / .rpm / .AppImage, -Android.apk), and release notes end with a "Which download do I need?" table

### Engine
* A game mode on setup (cooperative or competitive): competitive is refused with a 'coming soon' message until it is built, and card scripts can already write their 'In competitive mode' clauses
* Team membership groundwork for competitive mode: a cooperative game is one team of every seat, and a per-team player count is ready for two teams
* Per player values (villain hit points, scheme threat, hinder, uses, minion hit points, printed cost and per player amounts in card text) are read through the team of the card they are printed on, ready for competitive mode; a cooperative game gives the same numbers as before.
* Team words for card scripts: the enemy leader, your leader, your team and the enemy team, read through the team queries (cooperatively the villain, no card, and the players)
* A choice a card gives to "the enemy team" is asked of the first player, marked as the enemy team's on the prompt and in the log (competitive plan task 7)
* A game can be set up with chosen stages of its main scheme card, played in the chosen order (GameSetupConfig.mainSchemeStageIndexes), for Civil War's pick of one stage 1 and one stage 2
* A Civil War leader takes three or four modular sets, refuses a set or a main scheme stage of the other side, and turns a chosen stage 1 and stage 2 into the main scheme deck (spec 3.1a)
* wave10Scenario builds any legal Civil War scenario (leader, stages, modular sets, difficulty, game mode), playableScenario routes to it, and the scenario offer lists sides, leaders, stage options, set legality and game modes (spec 3.1b)
* A defeat effect can ignore the defeated card's When Defeated abilities (Coup de Grâce), and a defeat made by an attack-labeled ability counts as that attack
* An encounter attachment can carry a per-host limit and a host chosen by the enemy team: a character at the limit is not a legal host at the reveal, and the host prompt is the first player's, marked as the enemy team's
* A defeated minion can be given to an enemy as a facedown boost card: setDefeatDestination(boostCardOf(enemy)), from an interrupt on the defeat or from the minion's own When Defeated ability (Teamwork minions, Dangerous Recruits)
* A response can hear a card revealed that gives heroes a trait: on.cardRevealedGranting(trait) with grantedTo(trait) for the cards given it (Hunting Rebel Heroes)
* A main scheme stage with hinder, printed or gained under a condition, has its hinder placed as it turns to its B side (wave 10 spec 3.13)
* An ability limit counted for each player the triggering event belongs to: once per phase per player on a forced trigger (wave 10 spec 3.14)
* An effect resolves the Boost ability of each minion in play (Freedom Fighters), with a named option for what the icons a borrowed ability gains do (spec 3.11)
* The unique rule turns away a revealed encounter attachment that matches a card in play before it attaches (spec 3.8)
* An attack in progress records that it uses the attacker's ATK (usedStat), carried onto its damage and its overkill damage; Predicate attackUsedAtk and the usesAtk mark on attack and dealDamage (wave 10 spec 3.17).
* RuleSpec attacksLoseKeywords: attacks do not have the named attack keywords against a matching character, whatever gave them (wave 10 spec 3.18).
* A permanent encounter attachment whose host leaves play, or whose player is eliminated, resolves its printed attach instruction again and the attaching is announced (wave 10 spec 3.19).
* Random Civil War scenarios: randomCivilWarScenario(seed, options) draws side, leader, both main scheme stages and the modular sets from a seed, with pins that a re-roll keeps
* Random Civil War scenarios: randomCivilWarScenario(seed, options) draws side, leader, both main scheme stages and the modular sets from a seed, with pins a re-roll keeps
* A constant can give each matching card the printed keywords of each other matching card (Earth's Mightiest Heroes).
* A card can replace a player character's attack or thwart before it happens, whether a basic power or an attack or thwart ability (Tangled Up).
* A rule that keeps cards out of play can be limited to one player, and the refused play names the card it is printed on (Teddy Altman).
* An encounter card can answer cards discarded from any player's deck and take them while the ability that discarded them still counts them (Skrull Business).
* Engine: an additional cost to play a card, and a cost that searches the encounter deck and its discard pile for a minion and puts it into play; Hunted (Tigra) is scripted (box heroes B3.1)
* Engine: what a resource generates can read the card being paid for; Alien Physiology (Hulkling) is scripted (box heroes B7.2)
* Engine: a resource whose cost takes a chosen amount of damage as it is spent, named in the payment and offered per amount in the payment prompt; Energy Siphon (Wonder Man) is scripted (hero packs H3.14)
* An identity's separate deck of encounter cards is built at setup and owned by its player (Hercules's Labor deck), so Hercules can be seated; such a card is an encounter card no player controls, in the deck and in play
* A script can reveal the top card of a separate deck (revealTopOfSeparateDeck) and count the cards left in it (separateDeckCountOf); an obligation enters its revealer's play area and an attachment with no attach line waits out of play for its own When Revealed
* A Labor card that leaves play goes to the bottom of the Labor deck unless it goes to the victory display; one revealed with no valid target is discarded, logged as revealedCardDiscarded, and ends on the bottom of the deck (RRG 1.8 FAQ p. 65)
* A maximum on the cards tucked under a card (`tuckLimit`, Ionic Physiology's "Max 3 cards tucked here"): a tuck takes only the cards there is room for, a full card refuses the tuck and skips the text after its "Then", and an ability that only tucks there is not offered
* Deck building: an identity may take off-aspect cards by printed resource (`OffAspectAllowance.printedResource`); Wonder Man's deck may hold events with a printed energy resource icon from any aspect, in `validateDeck` and in the deck builder's offer
* A leaves-play trigger can ask where the card went: `on.leavesPlay(who, { to: "victoryDisplay" })` hears a Victory card added to the victory display and not the same card discarded (Hercules' Atonement; hero packs HT7)
* A find can choose among every matching card, in play or out, each offered with the area it is in, and can reveal the card or put it into play: `findCard` gains `choose`, `outOfPlay` and the destinations `"reveal"` and `"play"`; every deck a match could be in is shuffled (Hercules' Labor cards; hero packs HT4)
* A reveal can treat each per player icon on the revealed card as a number, for that reveal only: `perPlayerAs` on `revealCard` and on a `findCard` that reveals (Embody Pathos; hero packs HT5, question 19 = A, provisional)
* Threat that only one character's thwarts can remove: `threatCannotBeRemoved` gains `exceptThwartBy` (`onlyThwartsRemoveThreat`), which stops every other removal, encounter cards' and supports' included (Embody Pathos; hero packs HT6)
* A cost can be reduced to 0 for a player's next matching card (`reduceNextCardCost(player, "toZero", …)`), and a reduction can be bound to the cards a search found, following each card until its next play (`reduceCostOfCards`; hero packs HT8, HT9)
* A cost that heals damage from a character the payer picks (healCost), for Battlefield Benevolence: an enemy the effect could not confuse is no candidate
* An additional cost to attack one enemy (additionalAttackCost), for Brother vs. Brother: paid as a basic attack or an (attack) ability is made against it, and an enemy that cannot be paid for cannot be attacked
* A resource ability that can only be overpaid: it never goes toward the cost, is always counted as overpaid, and is offered only while a card is being played (hero packs HT11)
* A value that counts the cards in a player's hand, deck or discard pile matching a query, so an option that takes a card from the discard pile is offered only while one is there
* A collection search for an identity-specific card of an identity not in the game, and Setup abilities of player cards in play resolving at setup step 16 (hero packs HT15)
* Any player may help pay the costs on a card: a rule that opens an attached card's ability costs to every player's resources, discards and cards in play, and lets one other player take over a part such as damage, while a part naming the user's own card stays with them (hero packs HT16)
