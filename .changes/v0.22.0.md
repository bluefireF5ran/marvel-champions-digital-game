## [v0.22.0] - 2026-10-16
### Added
* Desktop: the villain phase docks on the right with the table and your hand still in view, and the interrupt window answers there (#129, #138)
* Tablet: in landscape the villain phase takes the villain row with your hero, play area and hand below; in portrait it sits above your hand with its buttons at the thumb
* Phone: the villain phase keeps your hand in view with USE and RESOLVE at the thumb, and PEEK AT TABLE lets you look at the board while it waits
* While the villain phase is up, each card in your hand is tagged with when it can answer it (Play now, When hit, On a treachery, When threat lands, Resource, Your turn), and the one you can play now is outlined in yellow (#129, #138)
* Desktop: rest the mouse on a card on the table to see it larger beside the pointer, with its state; new Card size on hover setting (off, 1.5x to 4x, default 2.5x), every card blown up to the same size
* The docked villain phase slides in as the phase begins and back out after its recap; through the next player phase a handle at the table's edge reopens that round's recap for review
* A global Card size setting (Fit to screen, Same size everywhere, Large, Extra large) draws cards larger on the table, which then scrolls under the top bar, your hand and the action buttons, in the villain phase, and in Decks, the deck builder, Deck check, Rules and the Civil War builder, with fewer columns and scrolling instead of cut-off cards
* Desktop: the card hover popover also works on the villain phase's cards and on the table beside the docked villain phase
* The villain phase opens by saying who drew what as the player phase ended, and the cards you drew are tagged New in your hand
* Tap your discard pile (or the encounter discard) to fan out every card in it at once, most recent first, on phone, tablet and desktop; hold or right-click still opens Inspect, and the pile's top card shows the hover preview
* Cards drawn into your hand now fly in from your deck one after another, and each hand slot stays empty until its card arrives (reduced motion skips it)
* Settings: Hero card popover and Villain card popover turn the hover preview on or off for those cards
* The villain phase answers a defend and shows its recap inside the docked panel, widening the dock (about half a desktop screen) or growing the tablet band with a compact hero row, animated; Expand opens the full-screen sheet or recap
* Optional interrupt and response windows say what is about to happen (or just happened), why now, and what confirming does; a decision during table setup docks beside the setup card; Setting up the table honors Card size and has the card hover popover
* Resting the mouse on a card in Decks & Collection, the deck builder, Deck check, the Rules card list, the campaign Aftermath picker or the Civil War builder opens the same larger card popover as on the table
* Kang's per-player areas label their villains and main schemes ("VS SPIDER-MAN"), and Sinister Six villains show an ORDER n chip
* A scheme activation names the scheme that gets the threat, and the recap header shows each side or extra main scheme that gained threat
* A change of active villain (Wrecking Crew, Four Horsemen) gets its own beat in the villain phase and its own recap row
* Ending a turn on a hand size under 4 asks "Are you sure?" and explains the hand size: the printed value, what changes it (Iron Man's +1 per Tech upgrade) and the other face's value; the hand caption shows the hand size beside the count (#150)
* Set the table: each modular set shows a picture of one of its cards and a Cards button that lists every card in the set (tap one to read it), and a Random button draws legal modular sets for you.
* The game's seed is now in the pause menu's status line, opens the game log, and is in the Ctrl+Shift+D diagnostic snapshot, so a game can be named exactly in a bug report or replayed.
* Set the table: a Cards button on the Standard set (and Expert set) row opens every version in one sheet, each saying where it comes from, and each seat shows its hero's picture.
* The first player token: it sits on the first player on Set the table and on the board, and is tossed (or slid, bounced, spun or placed, a new Settings option) to each new first player.
* The name-conflict sheet now offers "Replace with Team-Up" on a hero's own card that matches another seated hero, listing only the Team-Up cards that make the swap legal (RRG v1.8 p.50).
* A bug icon in the board's top bar (and on Title) opens Report an issue: a screenshot tip for your device, Copy log, Download log, and links to report a bug, suggest a feature or request a deck on GitHub (#176).
### Changed
* Villain-phase walkthrough now tracks the encounter cards dealt and revealed and tags each beat with its card; adds the pure held-open recap model.
* With Auto-advance off (the default) the docked villain phase waits for you to press Start villain phase, and a decision someone has to make comes up as a notice with Answer instead of a sheet jumping over your cards
* The hover preview of a scheme shows its threat as the board's red meter with the count, and every card hovers at the same size, the hero panel included
* The main scheme card grows with Card size: its rows scale, and above Fit to screen the whole sideways card is shown larger
* Set the table: unchosen difficulty cards, modular sets and options are drawn at full strength instead of faded (they looked unavailable), and the sidebar shows the chosen villain's picture.
* Settings is split into tabs (Game, Cards, Display, Guide, More) with labeled sections inside them, instead of one long scrolling list.

### Engine
* The end-of-phase draw reports each player's refill (handRefilled), a full hand too, and the log says so, so a small hand size no longer reads as a missed draw (#150)
* Media Coverage now repeats a revealed card's Incite and Surge as well as its printed When Revealed abilities, since both keywords are When Revealed abilities (RRG v1.8 p.24, p.42; ruling Aug 3, 2026 R3).
* A "deal N damage to a character" cost (Thwip Thwip!, Quick Quip, Spider-Tingle, Clarity of Purpose) now counts as paid even when Toughness, a tough status card or a prevention or reduction effect stops the damage (RRG v1.8 p.14; rulings June 25 and July 9, 2026).
* Deck legality now allows Appendix I's Team-Up replacement: a hero's own card that matches another seated hero can be swapped for a Team-Up card naming both (RRG v1.8 p.50).
* A stunned hero or ally can attempt a basic attack even when no enemy is a valid target; the attempt exhausts it and removes the stunned card without dealing damage (RRG v1.8 p.41).
* An attack's forced "after attacks / is attacked / defends / takes or deals damage" abilities, retaliate included, now all resolve before any non-forced response to the same attack (RRG v1.8 p.8-9).
* Peril now stops other players from triggering optional interrupts and responses, including (defense) events, while a player resolves a peril card, and from triggering abilities on a peril card in another player's area; forced abilities still resolve (RRG v1.8 p.32; ruling July 9, 2026 R3).
* While a player resolves a peril card, only that player's characters can defend (RRG v1.8 p.32; ruling July 9, 2026 R3).
* An ally whose basic attack or thwart target leaves play before it resolves no longer takes consequential damage, and its "after it attacks/thwarts" abilities no longer trigger (RRG v1.8 p.13).
* A stunned character that cannot attack at all (for example Vision in Intangible form) can no longer use a basic attack attempt to remove its stun, and "you cannot make basic attacks" is now always found when other attack prohibitions are in play (FFG ruling Jan 18, 2023; RRG v1.8 p.11).
* A stunned character that cannot attack (for example Vision in Intangible form) can no longer use an attack ability to remove its stun (FFG ruling Jan 18, 2023; RRG v1.8 p.11).
* A boost card turned faceup now resolves its interrupts, then its Boost ability, then its responses (RRG v1.8 p.5), so Target Acquired is no longer offered once the Boost ability has resolved.
* Return the Favor: when the treachery revealed while paying its cost stuns your hero, the stun is discarded instead of dealing the 5 damage, and the cost stays paid (FFG ruling April 30, 2026).
* Damage now resolves in RRG v1.8 p.14 order: "would be dealt" abilities such as Armored Rhino Suit resolve before tough status cards, so Rhino keeps his tough card while the suit takes the hit, and "would take" abilities still lose to tough.
* Every response triggered while an encounter card is being revealed (incite or When Revealed threat, damage, cards entering play, discards, status cards) now waits until the reveal has finished, and a revealed character's toughness arrives after its When Revealed (RRG v1.8 p.38, p.45).
* Surge now deals its facedown encounter card while the surging card's When Revealed abilities resolve, so responses to that card's reveal can no longer change which card surge dealt (RRG v1.8 p.42).
