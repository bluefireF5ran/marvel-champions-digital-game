## [v0.23.0] - 2026-10-17
### Added
* Desktop app: Settings > Display > Window picks Windowed, Borderless or Fullscreen and, from a dropdown with Apply, a resolution (Native by default). On Windows, Fullscreen really switches the monitor to one of its own display modes and puts yours back when you leave Fullscreen, switch away, quit or crash; elsewhere the game draws at that resolution and scales to fit with black bars. SteamOS and Bazzite start in Fullscreen at native, with a Steam Deck row to return to it.
* Desktop app: a Quit to desktop button on the Title screen.
* Music player (#191): a music button in the table's and Title's top bar announces each new song with a Now playing pill (a notification on phones) and opens a player with your earned songs, Shuffle and Next; Extras' Music tab gains an expanded player with a progress bar; Settings ▸ Sound adds Shuffle earned music.
* New in Extras (#191): a tink and a notice naming what a game just opened (New song earned, New villain file, 3 new extras unlocked) slide in as it happens.
* A sound plays when hands refill at the end of the player phase.
* A punch sound plays on every basic attack.
* Sounds for a basic thwart, a card played or entering play, and a Spell played; a card's sound plays before its attack or thwart.
* Doctor Strange's Cloak of Levitation and Captain America's Shield Toss have their own sounds.
### Changed
* Arrow keys and the D-pad move focus to the nearest control in that direction on screen; Tab and Shift+Tab keep the route order.
* Pull requests from forks now run the typecheck and unit tests on CI; only the Netlify preview deploy still skips them.
* Netlify builds deploy previews only for pull requests from forks; everything else still deploys from GitHub Actions.
### Fixed
* Android: the update notice and other outside links open in your browser instead of the GitHub app's in-app tab, where the APK download stalled and its Open button did nothing
* Android: the update banner's Download and the game's other outside links open again (the browser hand-off never finished)
* Conceding from the pause menu while the villain phase waits for Start now ends the game and goes to Game Over, instead of leaving the round stuck behind a menu reading Defeat.
* The pause menu's status line reads Conceded after a concession, not Defeat.
* The keyboard and controller focus ring now frames the scenario and hero cards it selects (it sat one gutter to the left on both setup screens).
* Sound effects start on the action: the silence at the front of the punch, card, Spell and Cloak sounds is trimmed.
* Sound effects load when the game starts, so the first deal or punch of a session is not late.
### Docs
* A CODEOWNERS file asks the maintainer to review every pull request.
