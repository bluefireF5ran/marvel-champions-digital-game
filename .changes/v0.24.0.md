## [v0.24.0] - 2026-10-18
### Added
* The deck builder shows the card pool as card pictures (List view keeps the text rows), searches card text, warns before leaving unsaved changes, can delete a saved deck, and New deck picks a hero from searchable tiles and starts from an empty deck or a precon copy (#217).
* Decks & Collection gets a card search, an Edit a copy button for precons and a confirm step on Delete (#217).
* Take your seats shows one tile per hero with a count of its decks and a Pick a deck submenu; search matches deck names, authors and notes, ranked by relevance and run in a background worker (#219).
* Every decision sheet can step aside to show the table: Peek at table on the full-screen sheets (your turn's targets and discards, the villain phase's defend and choices), Hide or Peek on the docked defend, and Collapse to dock a decision again after Expand (#231).
### Changed
* Every search filter (Take your seats, Scenario select, Decks & Collection, the deck builder and the campaign roster and collection) ranks its matches in a background worker, fuzzy and by relevance, and the worker shuts down when idle.
* A revealed card's decision during the villain phase (Hydra Bomber's choose one) now docks in the villain phase panel beside the table, with Hide on desktop and tablet and Peek at table on phone, so you can check the battlefield before you choose (#231).
* CI: one "All checks" check covers lint, format, typecheck, the unit tests and the client build, so the branch ruleset can require it alone; lint moved from checks.yml into the CI workflow
### Fixed
* The defend prompt's damage range now counts prevention already on the attack, such as Magic Barrier's "prevent 3" (#212).
* A decision sheet no longer keeps a card picked on a sheet that was undone: after an undo, the end-of-turn discard could hold an invisible pick that stopped the hand's last card from being chosen (#211).
* Clicking a shelf's scroll arrow (Take your seats, Choose a scenario, Extras, campaign shelves) no longer also picks the card that slides in under it (#218)
* Editing a precon now edits your own copy: saving it adds a new deck instead of silently keeping the precon, and decks saved the old way are repaired into their own decks (#217).
* Domino: a card discarded from the top of her deck still counts for the card that discarded it after its response moves it, so Jackpot! shuffled back gives Luck Be a Lady, A Good Workout and the rest its three icons (Digging Deep, White Fox and The Painted Lady likewise; Age of Apocalypse mission attempts keep FFG's ruling and warn). The response sheet names what discarded the card (#222).
* Opening the app, the Campaign saga and Decks & Collection no longer freezes for seconds on older devices: release builds skip the card-script check, art decodes off the main thread and arrives in one redraw, and deck checks are cached (#223)
* Phones: a sideways swipe of the play area that lifts on the villain phase review handle no longer opens the recap, and the play area no longer runs under the handle (#227)
* Battle Fury, Jarnbjorn, Tigra and every other "after you attack (and defeat) a minion" response now trigger when the attack defeats an Ultron Drone or another card that is a minion only while in play (#228)
* Phone: during a payment, a tap on a pinned table card (Peter Parker's Scientist) no longer picks the hand card scrolled under it.
* Phone: the pause menu sets Card size in place as a quick setting, and a Show all settings row opens the full Settings screen; before, a phone could reach only a few Table toggles mid-game.
* Phone: on the full-screen defend sheet, a drag that lifts on an option no longer picks it.
