## [v0.25.0] - 2026-10-18
### Added
* The music player has Pause, and on desktop a volume slider; Settings > Display > Sound has Music and Effects volumes, and in the Windows app the slider and a Game volume row follow the game's own entry in the Windows volume mixer (#243).
### Changed
* Opening the app and the first visit to Decks & Collection, Campaign and Extras freeze less on slower devices: screens load on demand, labels are measured once per font and painted off the main thread, and deck checks run in a worker
* Settings has a Sound tab: Music and Effects volumes on every platform, Game volume in the Windows app; muting the computer pauses the music (#243).
### Fixed
* Dauntless and Fighting Fit compare remaining hit points with the printed starting hit points, so a damaged hero with a hit point upgrade keeps the benefit (RRG 1.8 p. 22).
* Superhuman Strength no longer discards itself when the attacked enemy cannot be stunned (already stunned, stalwart) or was defeated by the attack (RRG 1.8 FAQ p. 57; FFG rulings May 25 and 30, 2023)
* Hela's second villain face (A2) now counts as stage 2, so effects that read her stage number see 2 (FFG ruling, Sep 5, 2024)
* Warning no longer has the Defense trait (RRG 1.8 p. 65 errata)
* Events with a cost before the arrow (Defender of the Nine Realms, Return the Favor, Karmic Blast, Cosmic Awareness) now pay that cost when the hero is stunned or confused, and Cosmic Awareness needs 3 threat on a scheme (FFG ruling April 30, 2026 (2); RRG 1.8 p. 14).
* We Are One can no longer be discarded by spending wild resources as energy, mental and physical: a "printed resources" cost does not let a wild stand in for another type (RRG p. 35).
* Spectrum tucking a card with two matching resource icons (such as Mental and Wild) now gets both bonuses, e.g. +4 THW (FFG ruling, August 22, 2025).
* Mutant Education can no longer be played with no identity-specific card in your discard pile and no X-Mansion in play (FFG ruling May 22, 2023; RRG 1.8 p. 42).
* Worried Father and Lost Visor: the Alter-Ego Action adds George Stacy or Ruby Quartz Visor to hand from wherever the card is when the search found nothing (FFG ruling May 10, 2024, Ruling 180).
* Seduced (Enchantress set 21179 and Valkyrie nemesis 25032) now bars only basic attacks, not attacks made by card abilities (RRG p. 10, p. 49).
* X-Men Instruction can no longer be played with no X-MEN ally in your discard pile and no X-Mansion in play (FFG ruling May 22, 2023; RRG 1.8 p. 42).
* Deadpool (ally) healing 3 damage when consequential damage would defeat him no longer skips the defeat check: if he still has no hit points left he is defeated (FFG ruling, March 15, 2024; RRG 1.8 p. 14).
* Cosmo: naming a card type now offers player side scheme for a player deck and environment for the encounter deck, plus encounter types for a player deck holding an encounter card (RRG 1.8 p. 12, errata p. 66).
* Chill Out! played by another hero can attach Iceman's set-aside Frostbite (FFG ruling of June 20, 2024, Q216 answer 2).
* Deckbuilding: Spider-Woman's identity-specific cards that print an aspect (Pheromones) now count toward that aspect in her equal split, so 10 Leadership + 12 'Pool is legal (FFG ruling Mar 22, 2024)
* "I Got This" checks the icons in play when it is played and lets you resolve its bullets in the order you choose (FFG ruling, post-RRG 1.5).
* Every ability labeled "(attack)" is now an attack by your identity from when it begins resolving, even when it deals no enemy damage (Multitalented paid without [physical] still counts as attacking), RRG p. 26 and FFG ruling 69.67
* Hex Bolt: you choose the order in which the discarded cards resolve, each counting its own boost icons as it resolves (FFG ruling, Oct 26, 2023).
* Hulk (ally) discarding a card with several printed resources, such as Salvage, now lets you choose the order the results resolve in (RRG 1.8 p. 61).
* Nebula's Combat Protocols discards only the Technique upgrades whose Special actually resolved; one replaced by a stunned or confused status card stays in play (RRG 1.8 p. 44; FFG ruling Oct 18, 2024).
* Side Holster makes room for Laser Swords, so Captain America's Shield, Side Holster and Laser Swords stay in play together (FFG ruling 152, March 22, 2024)
* Laser Swords (and Kurt's Cutlasses) count as one of your restricted cards weighing 2: over the restricted limit they can be chosen for the discard, freeing 2 (RRG p. 38; contributor's rules call (bluefireF5ran) 2026-10-16)
* A character that cannot be healed is no longer offered as the target of a heal, and an ability whose only effect is healing such a character cannot be used (RRG p. 43; ruling Dec 8, 2023)
* A piercing attack (Piercing Strike, or a basic attack that gains piercing) can target a Tough enemy that cannot take damage and discards its Tough card (RRG p. 43; ruling Jul 11, 2025)
* A character with a printed dash THW (such as the Hulk ally) can no longer thwart The Red House with its ATK (ruling June 17, 2025; RRG p. 15 "Dash")
* Energy Transfer (and any cost that attaches a card) can no longer name the character the card is already attached to (FFG ruling May 10, 2024, post-RRG 1.5 #182)
* Venom Goblin: the glider counter can no longer stay on its own scheme when another main scheme ties it for least (or most) threat; it must move to the other scheme (RRG p. 30 "Move", ruling of July 2, 2023)
* Hela's expert B2 side counts as stage 2, so Muster Courage and other stage-reading effects see 2 there too.
* Cosmo offers the same card types for every player deck, so naming a type no longer reveals whether that deck holds an encounter card; the Nebula pack reprint now matches the Star-Lord pack's Cosmo.
* A cancelled enemy attack still ends, so its "at the end of this attack" effects resolve: Rhino's Charge is discarded when Grasping Tendrils cancels his attack (FFG Ruling 148)
* "At the end of this attack" effects resolve before the attack's responses, so Riposte's delayed damage lands before Electrostatic Armor's 1 (RRG 1.8 "Delayed Effect"; FFG ruling Sep 26, 2024)
* A permanent player attachment whose host leaves play resolves its "attach to" text or is removed from the game (RRG 1.8 "Permanent"), except Frostbite, which is set aside, and Wrist Navigator when its host is defeated
* Wonder Man's attack asks which card to discard instead of discarding the first card in hand (#242).
* Ant-Man's Giant form shows the Giant card on the table and in the hover popover, and its type line reads GIANT (#244).
* CI parses every changelog fragment on pull requests, so a malformed one no longer reaches main and breaks the release PR
* Music keeps playing when the desktop app is in the background, and the game shows in the Windows volume mixer as Marvel Champions (#243).
### Docs
* Changelog fragments are linted by pnpm check, the pre-commit hook and their own CI check, so a malformed one shows up on its pull request
* Every YAML file is linted with yamllint by pnpm check, the pre-commit hook and its own YAML lint CI check
