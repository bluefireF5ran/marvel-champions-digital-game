## [v0.26.0] - 2026-10-18
### Added
* The Android app notices power saving mode: while it is on, the game turns on reduced motion, draws text at 1x and caps the frame rate at 30 fps (unless you set those yourself), and says so in a notice, on the title screen and in Settings. Settings > Display > Lighter play in power saving turns it off (#249).
* On Android the music pauses while the game is in the background and plays again when you come back to it (#249).
