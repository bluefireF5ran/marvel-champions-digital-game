## [v0.27.0] - 2026-10-19
### Added
* Ironheart's Level Up! gets its own band across the table, in her armor's colors, naming the version she becomes.
### Fixed
* An attack dealt two boost cards (Klaw) no longer lets a second press on Let it resolve pass the next boost card's interrupt window unseen: each window names its boost card and ignores presses for a moment after it opens, and a card on top of Magik's deck reads as played from the deck.
* Paying for a card no longer freezes the game for seconds on a slower device: a rules check that ran on every move listed every card ability in the game each time (#256).
* Dragging through the campaign roster's deck picker on a phone no longer closes it when the finger lifts; only a tap on the backdrop does (#257).
* Paying for a card no longer freezes the game: each check of your picks runs in the engine worker, and pressing Pay no longer stalls for over a second while the guide checks for a wasted card (#258).
* The deck builder's hero list shows Ironheart once instead of once per version card (#260).
* Ending a swipe over the hero-down banner on a phone no longer dismisses it; only a tap does.
* Starting Galaxy's Most Wanted mission 3 no longer deals each player a second opening hand (#263).
* Leaving Game over by Back to title after a campaign game no longer loses the result: continuing the campaign opens that game's Aftermath instead of the same mission (#265).
