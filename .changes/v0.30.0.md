## [v0.30.0] - 2026-10-21
### Added
* A resource that pays only for one kind of card, like Finesse, shows in the payment row as unable to pay for other cards. Inspect says why, and the pause menu's table rules explain aspect cards.
* The card sheet for a main scheme has a Show A side / Show B side button, so you can read the side you missed
### Changed
* A copied or downloaded bug report log puts its replay and debug snapshot in json code blocks, so they read as code when pasted into an issue.
### Fixed
* An effect that puts a "Hero form only" or "Alter-Ego form only" card into play no longer does so for a player in the other form; the card stays where it was (RRG p. 32)
* An effect that removes an acceleration token can no longer take one from the main scheme; it removes only a token placed on another card (RRG p. 5)
* An "after [enemy] attacks and damages" or "attacks and defeats" ability now resolves once for each character the attack damages or defeats, so overkill damage spilled onto an identity or villain triggers it again (Jigsaw, Toad, Cypher, Captain Marvel; ruling January 11, 2026 (2), RRG p. 31)
* Any player can now use the Hero Action of Obedience Potion and The Poison (RRG 1.8 p. 66 errata)
* A card whose text box is blank (Hope Summers treated as a minion) still loses the game when it leaves play: alternate win or loss conditions cannot be blanked (RRG p. 48; ruling Nov 29, 2023)
* "Attacks and damages you" (Green Goblin, Tombstone, the Sinister Six, Beetle, Gamora, Space Pirates, Power Gauntlets, Ionic Boots) now need your identity damaged, not a defending ally, "You'll Pay for That!" counts only your identity's damage and is not offered when your identity took none, and Defiance cannot be played while your ally defends (RRG p. 43, p. 49; FFG rulings Jul 18, 2024 and Mar 15, 2024)
* A sustained-damage cap such as Boarding Party lowers damage already above it as soon as it is in effect again, e.g. after a blank ends (FFG ruling Oct 10, 2025)
* Revealing another player's obligation while that player is in a different game area (Kang stage 2B) now removes it from the game and reveals a replacement card (FFG ruling Sep 21, 2023; RRG 1.8 p. 30)
* Bulldozer's Helmet resolves once per attack and discards 1 card per point of damage the whole attack dealt, overkill onto your hero included; Major Domo and Head of Steam count overkill damage too, and all three count damage a tough status card stopped, since it was dealt (RRG 1.8 p. 14, p. 31, p. 49; rulings Jan 11, 2026 (2) and Jan 17, 2026 (3))
* Battery Pack moves its charge counter rather than removing and adding one, so it arrives as the type the Tech card defines (a reflection counter on Energy Barrier), per RRG p. 6 and the Battery Pack FAQ on p. 62
* Finesse's resource can only be used while Spider-Woman is in hero form, as its Hero Resource text says.
* The Mad Titan's Shadow campaign Cosmo reward shows its own card art instead of the basic Cosmo's (#296).
* Main schemes show their B side in play and their real A side as the other side: Mad Titan's Shadow schemes stayed on their A side, and many later packs showed the B side on both
* The guide's payment warning suggests the cheapest card to spend instead (a free resource ability, then a resource card, then a card with no play this round), says why it picked it, checks resource types, and no longer calls a chosen spend an overpay (#299).
* The defend picker shows each defender's health now beside what the attack leaves it ("Now 2 of 4 HP → 0 left"), so a hurt ally reads as hurt, and its card pictures open the hover popover (#298).
* Hovering a defender's picture in the defend picker shows its health bar with the attack's damage hatched ("2→0"), as the villain's own preview does (#298).
* On a narrow desktop window the defend picker writes each defender's health as "3/3 HP → 0–1 LEFT" instead of cutting the line off (#298).
* Spectrum's energy forms show faceup, in their own column beside the play area, and the form picker shows each form's face instead of a card back (#308).
* A card hover popover on a screen with a search field (Seats, Decks, the deck builder) no longer slides under the field.
### Engine
* The pay step's query lists resource abilities that can't pay for the card only because they generate for another kind of card (Finesse for a hero card).
### Docs
* Rule comments cite the FFG rulings issued after March 2025 by date, as Hall of Heroes lists them, instead of a made-up "69.NN" number
