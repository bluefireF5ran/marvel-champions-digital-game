## [v0.31.0] - 2026-10-21
### Added
* Daredevil and Echo, the Fear No Evil heroes, are fully scripted with their starter decks
* Fear No Evil campaign is playable in the client: choose each scenario from the progress draw, set the villain order, and Daredevil and Echo's starter decks unlock with the box after the Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. campaign.
* Earned item: Hall of Heroes' Ultron Drone art, earned with the Drone On achievement (win a game against Ultron). Facedown Drones then show a Drone card with their ATK; turn it on or off in Settings > More > Earned items (#291).
* A hidden-hand facedown pick shows each option as a player card back under a "Choose cards from X's hand" title, selectable but never hovered or inspected.
* Fear No Evil's six scenarios (with a villain picker at setup), Jessica Jones and Luke Cage's starter decks, and their art join the pool.
* Achievements model: 232 achievements across wins, villains, campaigns, heroes, Expert, feats, team-ups and funny losses, derived from the save history, with lifetime champion points and nine ranks (screens to follow).
* Trophy Room: every achievement with its progress under a rank header, reached from the Title and Extras; achievement points add to your champion points.
* Title rank badge (rank, lifetime points, bar to the next rank) that opens the Trophy Room, with a Rank up banner and sound on a new rank; Extras can unlock on an achievement.
* A finished game's save now records a small summary (loss reason, heroes defeated, winners' hit points, allies played), so the Respawn Pending, Read the Room, Cutting It Close, I Work Alone and Gamma-Ray Overdose achievements count from new games on.
* Art & Credits screen (from Extras): who made the card scans, art, comic pages, music and tools, under the fan-project notice.
* Scenario select shows villain art for the five Fear No Evil scheme scenarios and the She-Hulk and Vision leaders, from FFG's announcement art (via Hall of Heroes), instead of a card scan.
* An "Achievement unlocked" popup with its own sound when you earn an achievement; Settings > More > Achievements sets how long it stays up (or turns it off) and where it shows.
* Settings > More > Achievements has a Popup size (Small, Medium, Large), and the popup's trophy badge now fits the MC logo inside the cup.
* New achievement "Studying Like Doctor Strange" for finishing all of How to Play (every basics lesson and every aspect's Try it).
* Achievements for playing a game as each hero, each named for the hero's line ("Another!", "I Am Iron Man", "Wakanda Forever!"), and "Sometimes You Gotta Run Before You Can Walk" for turning on Unlock everything.
* Achievement "I Love You 3000" for dealing the final blow to Thanos as Iron Man; a finished game's save now records whose card landed the last blow.
* Game Over shows the Fear No Evil villain you faced winning or losing (Hammerhead, Bullseye, Purple Man, Typhoid Mary) and Kingpin defeated; Kingpin's transfer is in Extras.
* Winning the Kingpin finale with Daredevil at the table shows Kingpin's transfer with Matt Murdock on Game Over.
* Achievements for deck building (Some Assembly Required, Taking It for a Spin, Mark II), for finishing a game with one to four seats filled, and Better Together for your first Team-Up card.

* Zeal for the Cause checks whether you are engaged with an Acolyte minion, as printed.

* When an encounter card picks one of several eligible cards without printing 'choose' (including 'a card you control' and ties on highest/lowest), the first player now picks (RRG 1.8 'First Player', p. 19); obligations and Peril stay with their player.
* A flipped double-sided encounter attachment applies its showing face's printed stat modifiers (Vision's Dense and Intangible, Hope's Captor).
* Ties on 'the villain/scheme with the most or least threat' (Wrecking Crew, Crossfire) are broken by the first player, and a tied side-scheme pick no longer puts threat on every tied scheme.
* The Civil War builder lays out three leaders per side and five stage options, so it opens with Synthezoid Smackdown's leaders; Random can draw them.
* Metal Bracer reduces damage as it would be dealt, before tough (FFG ruling Aug 26, 2026); Kingpin keeps his protections as an ally under Karma (Sep 10); King's Gambit shuffles even with an empty discard pile (Sep 10).
* 'When a card is revealed from the encounter deck' (Black Widow, Enhanced Spider-Sense and 12 more) no longer hears reveals from under a card, the set-aside area or a search (FFG ruling Sep 14, 2026); Return the Favor's treachery still counts as from the encounter deck.
* Encounter options that stun or confuse a fixed identity are no longer offered when that identity can't take the status (RRG p. 42), and Deadly Contraptions' Disarm options are offered whenever their discard can resolve.
* An Ultron Drone that attacks and is then defeated in the same villain phase is still called a Drone in the log and the round recap, instead of naming the card underneath (#291).
* Regression test: a deck left under 40 cards when Fear No Evil removes a recorded title still blocks the next game, and the removed title cannot be added back.

* fetch_card_art.py's trim no longer turns white text boxes see-through (with a --self-test and an interior-transparency check in audit), and the 25 more scans it flagged are solid again.
* The defend picker's health line wraps instead of being cut off when four defenders are offered.
* A player side scheme in your hand is drawn sideways, showing the whole card, instead of cropped into a portrait slot.
* Fear No Evil now plays an issue's comic after you pick that issue on the Briefing (it was skipped because the issue is chosen there), and numbers issues in the order you play them.

### Engine
* Daredevil's Sense deck keeps its order: a fixed-order separate deck is shuffled only at setup, and only its owner may look through it
* A card can be played from a separate deck (its top card, or any card as a search), and a play that ignores the resource cost can put an upgrade on a host the player chooses
* Scripting registries for Jessica Jones and Luke Cage, with five reprints playable through their originals' scripts
* A card can go to the bottom of its owner's separate deck instead of leaving play, alone or as an attachment leaving with its host
* Unpreventable damage (Luke Cage): it ignores tough status cards and damage reductions and preventions; increases, redirects, caps and "cannot take damage" still apply
* An interrupt can answer the removal of a scheme's last threat, before the threat is removed and before the scheme is defeated
* A scheme's threat can be limited to removal by one identity and its events and upgrades (Focus the Senses), and an event is the playing player's, not its owner's
* The Starting keyword: before drawing the starting hand a player may take their Starting card, which counts toward hand size, and a deck holds at most one Starting card
* Exactly defeating a side scheme: a thwart that removes no more threat than the scheme holds
* A card tucked under another can be played as if it were in hand, on every play-from-hand route
* A card can let you play a tucked event as if from hand by discarding itself from your hand (Photographic Reflexes), and a card with a star cost is never played
* A character can use one stat in place of others (The Best Offense: DEF for THW and ATK, ignoring their modifiers), and an upgrade can say it attaches to minions or side schemes
* After a defense card redirects an enemy attack, the defending player declares the defender; and a card can remove any acceleration token in play
* A card can skip the next villain phase (the round still ends), and round-end abilities now trigger on the round's end even when the villain phase is skipped
* Lasting effects that end with the player phase, and a stat equal to the highest among the other characters (Purple Man)
* "Attach to a card" upgrades, end-of-thwart effects that read whether the thwart defeated the scheme, a hit points value, a check that a card in hand can be played, and Take a Stand's bonus only inside a player phase
* A cost can choose a card in play, and a counter cost's amount can be read from the board after that choice (4K Digital Camcorder); a deck play "as if in hand" counts as from hand (Calling in Favors)
* A basic thwart made on a card's instruction can be the hero's own, made without exhausting and ignoring patrol and crisis (Grapnel Launcher); a damage reduction can be read live, such as your hero's DEF (Echo)
* Attacks against a character can gain a keyword and deal extra damage (Cruisin' for a Bruisin'), and Prerequisite is enforced when a card is played (Defend Our City)
* A query for cards that print a Boost ability, and a search for allies named on a Team-Up card (Sidewinder, Dynamic Duo)
* An ability cost can change your form (Entrapment), paid together with any additional cost to change form, and player side schemes are matched by card type
* Change-form costs no longer depend on remembered objects; `changeFormCost` is refused on resource abilities, and the client labels it "change to hero form".
* `reduceCostToPlay(query, n, player?)`: a play-cost rule that can last until the end of the phase (Defend Our City).
* Attachment hosts honour 'without a [trait] attachment' (withoutAttachmentTrait), so a Vehicle skips an enemy that already has one.
* 'Cannot be dealt damage' (cannotTakeDamage.stage: "dealt"): the damage is never dealt, so no 'would be dealt' interrupt, tough or piercing, and no 'after ... dealt' response; logged and shown as damageNotDealt.
* A main scheme per player in their own play area (Protection Racket): setup picks or draws one per seat, 'the main scheme' is read by play area, enemies scheme onto the activated player's scheme, acceleration tokens go near the villain and add to each, and an anyPlayerEliminated predicate.
* attacksLoseKeywords can be scoped to the attacker ("Bullseye's attacks lose ranged"), so the loss beats any grant of the keyword; the defend preview reads ranged the way the attack does.
* Main scheme swap (Change of Venue): swapCards exchanges a main scheme in play with a set-aside one in the same play area at 1B (attachments discarded, the old one set aside), optional placePreviousThreat, a 'your main scheme' target; logged mainSchemeSwapped.
* A villain can start on a random side (setVillainSide, drawn from the seed; Disturbed Psyche), encounter cards' Setup abilities resolve in setup step 12, and a villain face revealed by a flip gets its toughness (MC60 p. 26 FAQ).
* gainsAbility can grant a Boost ability to boost cards (TargetQuery.boostCard, DSL boostCardsFromSet), resolved after the card's printed Boost abilities when it is turned faceup (Purple Man's set).
* Add an eventKindIs predicate, so one ability that answers several kinds of event can do something different for each; replacement threat placements and removals keep the original's source and the thwart or activation they belong to; EventPattern.removalWillApply ignores removals that will be stopped.
* A minion can hold threat and be treated as an ally by its own constant while a condition holds (treatSelfAsAlly, takeControl; Black Cat 60169); a bare 'when threat is placed' interrupt now hears only threat placed on schemes.
* Black Cat's ally treatment holds at exactly 4 threat (owner Q205 = B): a 5th threat turns her back into a minion.
* Each ally a query matches can be treated as a minion while it matches (treatAsMinion: Baron Zemo's 'each ally with threat on it'), with SCH equal to its base THW; it is an ally again under its owner's control, keeping its threat, once the query stops matching or the card leaves play or is blanked.
* Encounter minions can be tucked under a side scheme and revealed from there: a response can hear a card revealed from the encounter deck only, and a card's own When Defeated can tuck it under another card in place of its discard.
* Fear No Evil scenario builder wave11Scenario: the main scheme is the scenario and the villain a setup option (villainSetId), offered to setup screens as PlayableScenarioOffer.villains; per-player main scheme picks pass through as mainSchemeChoice.
* Campaign runner: a choice campaign's finale is reached only through its finale.when (MC60 p. 9 step 4); nodeProgress values; choices over Completed scenarios; removal from the campaign by title and subtitle (MC60 p. 9 step 8); cards removed from the campaign are never set aside.
* Villainous works for allies and heroes (RRG 1.8 p. 47; MC60 p. 26 FAQ, Black Cat 60169): a basic power is given a facedown boost card, turned faceup as the power resolves and heard by unqualified 'boost card turned faceup' cards; its Boost resolves for its controller, its icons go to the stat used (following any swap), and it is discarded once the power has resolved. An ally's defense gets no card.
* putIntoPlay can resolve the Setup abilities of the cards it puts into play (resolveSetup; the first player orders two or more, RRG p. 40) and put a double-sided card into play on its other face without flipping it (otherFace, logged cardEnteredOnOtherFace), for Fear No Evil's campaign environments.
* Civil War leaders choose main scheme stages from every stage of their side in either box (owner question SS1 open, built A); a gated box's stages are not offered.
* An encounter attachment attached by its attach instruction enters play once, so its Uses counters are placed once (Bear Hug starts with 3 grip counters, not 6).
* A 'would be given a facedown boost card' interrupt window (boostCardBeingGiven, on.boostCardWouldBeGiven) lets an ability replace the give of a boost card; the setAside card selector takes random, picked from the seeded RNG.
* A When Defeated or When Revealed ability a card gains now resolves: game-triggered abilities read the card's live abilities, printed and gained, so a blank text box keeps a gained one and an ignore-When-Defeated effect stops it (RRG 1.8 'Gains', p. 21).
* An attack's characterAttacked event records who the target was engaged with as the attack was made, so 'after … is attacked' patterns still see a target the attack defeated (Battle in the Bodega, owner Q208 open, built A); a main scheme swap compares faceup 1B titles (RRG 1.8 pp. 23, 42).
* A flipped double-sided ally uses its other face's printed THW, ATK and hit points (RRG 1.8 'Flip', p. 20): Bloody Mary, and Odin's King side.
* forEachPlayer can let the first player choose the order players resolve it in (order: firstPlayerChooses, DSL forEachPlayerInChosenOrder; RRG 1.8 'Each Player', p. 17); inEncounterSet also matches a support or ally printed as belonging to an encounter set (specificTo).
* forEachPlayer can bind its passes' results (bind): each player's vars and chosen cards are summed or joined back to the ability once every player has resolved.
* Campaign loss policy keepLog: a lost scenario keeps the campaign log and returns to the campaign's scenario choice instead of restoring the log and replaying the same scenario.
* resolveSetups effect: resolve the Setup abilities of cards already in play together, the first player choosing the order (RRG 1.8 'Simultaneous Resolution', p. 40). Campaign progress marks stop at the fail threshold.
* An ability counts as resolved only if one of its effects changed the game state (RRG 1.8 'Resolve', p. 37): Teenage Superheroes, Mighty Avengers, The Initiative, Freedom Fighters, Citywide Crisis, Elaborate Trap and Black Widow's Preparations now see a no-op as unresolved.
* A card cancelled as it is played (Spellcasting, Counterspell) is discarded before it enters play, so its own enters-play and after-played abilities never fire; it still counts as played for other cards (RRG 1.8 'Initiating Abilities', 'Cancel', p. 11).
* An ability whose effects change nothing no longer counts as resolved for 'resolved this way' or 'after you resolve' (RRG 1.8 'Resolve', p. 37).
* A scheme threat cannot be removed from is no target for a basic thwart while the rule is active; a confused hero may still try (RRG 1.8 'Target', p. 43).
* An 'if X, gets +N ATK/SCH for this activation' bonus is read after the activation's interrupts (Solar Beam, Swinging Stone, Unrelenting Savage; FFG ruling Feb 15, 2024).
* Ties on 'the [superlative] card in your hand' (Temporal Trickery, Thoughtcasting, View the Cosmos, Smoke Bombs) are broken by the first player, who picks among another player's tied cards facedown, in a shuffled order (owner 211B; RRG 1.8 pp. 19, 42).
* Scenario decks can be gathered by trait at setup (the Shadowland trap deck): discards route by printed trait, and a deck reshuffles its discard the moment it runs out, once per batch.
* A status card that cannot be placed (no room, or replaced by an 'instead' interrupt) means the effect did not fully resolve, so a following 'then' is skipped (RRG 1.8 'Then', p. 44).
* Reductions to 'the cost to play' an event also reduce its ability cost (FFG ruling Aug 26, 2026), so Photographic Reflexes lowers Make the Call's ally cost.
* Surge and incite count as When Revealed abilities when a card resolves another card's When Revealed, and the first player orders incite, printed When Revealed and surge on a reveal (FFG ruling Sep 22, 2026; Change of Venue and Countless Numbers' boosts excepted pending erratum).
* A card first dealt facedown to a player counts as revealed from the encounter deck (FFG rulings Jun 2, 2022 and Sep 14, 2026), for Enhanced Spider-Sense, Black Widow and SHOW's surge alike.
* Damage from an event or upgrade counts as coming from the identity that used it for 'damage from a character' checks (Krahllak's Blessing; RRG 1.8 'You, Your').
* Rogue's copied traits (Skin Contact, Energy Transfer, the Rogue ally) follow the chosen character and end when it leaves play, not when Touched moves (FFG ruling Oct 9, 2026).
