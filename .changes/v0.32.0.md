## [v0.32.0] - 2026-10-21
### Added
* Android Settings > Display > Screen: Resolution (Native down to 1x) and Frame rate (match the screen's refresh rate, 60 or 30 fps).
* Android picks Resolution and Frame rate defaults from the phone's hardware (memory, GPU, Android's performance class), and Settings says what it recommends.
### Fixed
* Gjallerbru and Hall of Nastrond (Hela) show their own card pictures instead of each other's (#314)
* The villain phase no longer stops on an encounter card after Flip Encounter Card on a phone in power saving mode, where the card stayed marked facedown with no Next card button (#315).
* The Android app draws at the phone's own resolution instead of stretching a 1x picture, so a 3x phone is sharp.
* The web and the desktop app draw at the display's own scale (Windows scaling, 4K, Retina) instead of a stretched 1x picture, with the same Render resolution and Frame rate settings.
### Docs
* Added Markdown copies of the post-RRG 1.6 FFG rulings (July 2024 to December 2025, split by month under docs/rulings/post-rrg-1-6/) and of the v1.6 Rules Reference.
