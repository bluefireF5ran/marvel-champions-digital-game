## [v0.34.0] - 2026-10-23
### Added
* Send to another device: Settings → Save data can send your saves to another phone, tablet or computer with a 12-character code or a QR code, encrypted on your device and gone after one use or 15 minutes (#276).
* Sync across devices: keep saved games, decks, campaigns and unlocks in step on your phone, tablet and computer with no login, set up once with a recovery code and linked by QR or a short code (#276).
* The Shadowland campaign: three scenarios in order, Dark Protégé villains checked off, Creeping Corruption and OVERPOWERING records, two recorded support or upgrade slots, and the expert persistent-damage rules.
* Creeping Corruption begins in play, one per player, in a standalone game with Black Flower as a modular set.
* Shadowland's setup screens: a random Dark Protégé villain (or a labelled house pick), Shadow Labyrinth's hidden set-aside draw, the trap deck and its discard, and the campaign's briefing and dossier.
* Shadowland (MC65): its three scenarios, seven encounter sets and campaign are playable, unlocked with Fear No Evil's Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. gate.
* Extras has a Side Stories tab. Its first comic, Thor and She-Hulk's date from Avengers #11, opens with the "Worthy of a Second Date?" achievement and reads panel by panel in the comic reader, each page ending on the whole page.
* Shadowland art goes live: a campaign cover, panel openers, Marvel's 2010 Shadowland comic pages between issues (credited on screen), scenario tile pictures, and earned Extras artwork.
* Shadowland: every card shows its own picture; full-size card images fill the faces the Hall of Heroes previews lacked.
### Changed
* Several games can be in progress at once: the title screen lists them with the device each was last played on, you can resume any of them or remove one, and starting a new game no longer retires the others (#276).
* On a phone held upright, the Inspect sheet shows the card scan at the full width of the screen, with its text and play history below (#358, thanks @zackburnley)
* With two or three villains (Tower Defense, the Sinister Six's opening pair), each villain is drawn as a full card panel with its stats and hit points, and an environment such as Avengers Tower gets a card-sized tile. An environment's damage shows as a large "1/9 DAMAGE" chip on every screen size (#356)
* The table-setup screen jumps (`screen=table-setup` and the screens after it) take `&seed=<n>` for a fixed deal, and the Shadow King setup spec uses it, so it no longer fails now and then when the hero loses before round 2.
* Shadowland expert campaign: each player's recorded hit points are set after the scenario's own setup (RRG Appendix II step 13), so setup damage no longer carries over.
* Swap 500 more card scans for sharper copies, most going from about 840px to 1030px tall
### Fixed
* An effect that puts a "Hero form only" or "Alter-Ego form only" card into play no longer does so for a player in the other form; the card stays where it was (RRG p. 32)
* An effect that removes an acceleration token can no longer take one from the main scheme; it removes only a token placed on another card (RRG p. 5)
* An "after [enemy] attacks and damages" or "attacks and defeats" ability now resolves once for each character the attack damages or defeats, so overkill damage spilled onto an identity or villain triggers it again (Jigsaw, Toad, Cypher, Captain Marvel; ruling January 11, 2026 (2), RRG p. 31)
* Any player can now use the Hero Action of Obedience Potion and The Poison (RRG 1.8 p. 66 errata)
* A card whose text box is blank (Hope Summers treated as a minion) still loses the game when it leaves play: alternate win or loss conditions cannot be blanked (RRG p. 48; ruling Nov 29, 2023)
* "Attacks and damages you" (Green Goblin, Tombstone, the Sinister Six, Beetle, Gamora, Space Pirates, Power Gauntlets, Ionic Boots) now need your identity damaged, not a defending ally, "You'll Pay for That!" counts only your identity's damage and is not offered when your identity took none, and Defiance cannot be played while your ally defends (RRG p. 43, p. 49; FFG rulings Jul 18, 2024 and Mar 15, 2024)
* A sustained-damage cap such as Boarding Party lowers damage already above it as soon as it is in effect again, e.g. after a blank ends (FFG ruling Oct 10, 2025)
* Revealing another player's obligation while that player is in a different game area (Kang stage 2B) now removes it from the game and reveals a replacement card (FFG ruling Sep 21, 2023; RRG 1.8 p. 30)
* Bulldozer's Helmet resolves once per attack and discards 1 card per point of damage the whole attack dealt, overkill onto your hero included; Major Domo and Head of Steam count overkill damage too, and all three count damage a tough status card stopped, since it was dealt (RRG 1.8 p. 14, p. 31, p. 49; rulings Jan 11, 2026 (2) and Jan 17, 2026 (3))
* Battery Pack moves its charge counter rather than removing and adding one, so it arrives as the type the Tech card defines (a reflection counter on Energy Barrier), per RRG p. 6 and the Battery Pack FAQ on p. 62
* Tower Defense: threat moved to "the main scheme" (Manipulated M.U.S.I.C.) goes only to the main scheme with Focused Defense (ruling Oct 10, 2025)
* Beat Cop moving threat off Show of Empathy puts the token on the non-Elite minion instead of also on Beat Cop (ruling Nov 21, 2025; RRG p. 30)
* A card that declares a character the defender no longer replaces a character already defending that attack (RRG 1.8 p. 15; FFG ruling Jul 19, 2023)
* An alter-ego is no longer "the character with the highest ATK" (Restrained), whether the card attaches to it or targets it: an alter-ego has no ATK, THW or DEF and a hero no REC (ruling April 17, 2025). Allies, minions and villains are still ranked by every stat
* An enemy attack moved onto a minion engaged with a player (Sinister Ends onto Hope Summers) now attacks that minion, and a player can still defend (ruling July 25, 2025)
* "Discard the lowest-cost upgrade you control" and other highest/lowest-cost removals skip a permanent card and take the next card that fits (Beetle, Speed Demon, Oversized Hands; RRG p. 32)
* Vapors of Valtorr can no longer target a Stalwart enemy to replace its tough status card, since it cannot be stunned or confused (FFG ruling Jun 20, 2024, Ruling 215; RRG p. 43)
* Replacing a status card with Vapors of Valtorr no longer counts as placing one, so Mister Sinister's Forced Response does not place threat (FFG ruling May 10, 2024, #181)
* With several main schemes in play (Venom Goblin), Cable, Montage and the Venom ally count the main scheme best for their controller each time, as the player may choose it (RRG 1.8 p. 63; FFG rulings of Dec 22, 2023 and May 16, 2024). In Tower Defense the Venom ally now reads the scheme with Focused Defense, as a player card's constant does (MC21 p. 10)
* Determination (22016) now asks which main scheme to remove threat from when several are in play (Venom Goblin, Tower Defense), as "the main scheme" on a player card lets its controller choose (MC27 insert, Venom Goblin FAQ; MC21 insert p. 10 for Tower Defense).
* A screen redrawn while a scrolled list is still gliding (the campaign market, once its card pictures load) no longer throws on the old list's scrollbar
* Mutant Protectors still exhausts its ally when another character is already defending the attack; the ally just cannot defend (RRG "And", p. 7; FFG ruling July 19, 2023)
* A card that discards your highest- or lowest-cost card and surges or offers another choice when you cannot (Inconspicuous Box, Oversized Hands, Breakthrough, Greycrow, Inconspicuous, Burn Notice) now treats a permanent card as nothing to discard (RRG p. 32)
* Arm Cannon's cost (discard the highest-cost upgrade you control) skips a permanent upgrade it cannot remove and takes the next-highest one (RRG p. 32)
* Exporting save data in the desktop app opens a Save dialog in Downloads instead of the share sheet.
* With reduced motion on (or battery saver, which turns it on), the villain phase stops on each facedown encounter card for Flip Encounter Card and on each revealed card for Next card again, instead of jumping straight to the recap (#342, reported by @zackburnley).
* Cards in a decision sheet open the card hover popover as soon as the sheet opens, not only after something redraws it.
* Optic Blast can be paid by Ruby Quartz Visor alone with an empty hand (#349, reported by @Lemantia).
* Defiance is offered while the boost card is still facedown, before it is turned up, as its "would be turned faceup" text asks (#348, reported by @Lemantia).
* Webbed Up replaces any attack your hero would make, an attack event or ability included, not only a basic attack (#346, reported by @Lemantia).
* Black Widow (54003), Jack Flag, the Flying and Psionic Inhuman allies and Passion for Justice are offered in alter-ego form too: their text says Response or Interrupt, not Hero Response or Hero Interrupt.
* Turn the Tide is offered when your hero thwarts the main scheme down to 0 threat, not only a side scheme.
* Pressing HIDE on the villain phase before the replay reaches your decision no longer leaves that decision hidden: the rail comes back for it, so an interrupt or a defense is not missed.
* Scarlet Spider and Spider-Tingle ("when you would reveal an encounter card") are offered while the card is still facedown, and Spider-Tingle only on your own reveals.
* A card in hand whose response only works once it is in play (Indomitable and other upgrades, supports and allies) is tagged Your turn during the villain phase, not When hit.
* The game log says when a card ability readies a character (Indomitable readying your hero), not only the cost it paid.
* Black Swan, Defensive Protocols, Retrieve Odin's Armor, Jormungand, Avengers Tower's damaged side, Odin's King side and the Norn Stone's flip side show their card art in The Mad Titan's Shadow campaign (#356)
* The attack target picker says which hero each minion is engaged with and its hit points, and lists targets in the same order as the villain area, so facedown Ultron Drones can be told apart (#357)
* Replace 1143 old card scans that still carried a faint FFG preview watermark, mostly Core Set, Red Skull, Wrecking Crew, Green Goblin and the early hero packs, with clean, sharper copies
* Green Goblin II (Risky Business) deals 4 indirect damage to each player when revealed, as printed, not 3 (#361, reported by @Perezh21).
* On a phone, a swipe across the table that starts on a card no longer opens Inspect on that card when the finger lifts
* Thwarting The Red House asks whether to use THW or ATK, so a character can thwart it with ATK as the card allows (#365, reported by @AlecHolland1582).
* On a phone, the Pause menu's ✕ closes it again after the settings rows are scrolled down; the same hidden-row leak is fixed on How to play, What's new in a box, aspect lessons, the guide panel, Inspect, compact Table setup and the saved-games list.
* Team-Up rings on a phone show the pair's picture instead of a black circle.
* A button half scrolled under a screen's header or footer (an aspect lesson's Try it, a Pause menu row) answers a tap on the part that shows again.
### Engine
* Scenario setup can choose its villain among the villain cards of a set-aside set (seeded random or picked), shuffling in that villain's linked cards and recording it as the chosen villain.
* A choice's options can carry a tag: a card can interrupt when a player chooses a tagged option, a constant can stop a player choosing one, and naming an encounter card type offers only encounter types.
* Card abilities can check which side of the villain is faceup.
* A main scheme's next stage counts as entering play when the scheme advances, so it no longer answers a thwart (or anything else) that happened against the stage before it.
* A set-aside villain card can be put into play as an Elite minion by the card attached to it, keeping its own text; when that card leaves it stays in play as a villain, a two-sided villain card discarded as a minion is removed from the game, and finding a villain searches every deck and discard pile or reveals one already in play.
* A villain flipping to its other side no longer gets a tough status card from Toughness; a new villain stage still does, and Typhoid Mary's flip (MC60 FAQ) and Shadow Labyrinth's expert side B reveal keep it.
