## [v0.36.2] - 2026-10-24
### Fixed
* On a narrow phone, an interrupt window during a villain attack (Backflip) no longer cuts the damage total off the attack's breakdown; a cut line now leads with it ("DAMAGE 3 = BASE 2 + …"), the window's tags stack instead of running off the panel, and a green stamp on the window says how much damage the interrupt prevents ("PREVENTS 2 DAMAGE"), with the hero's hit points now and if it resolves (#394, reported by @zackburnley).
* Hunting Down Heroes, The Infinity Stone, The Island of Dr. Zola, The Rise of Red Skull, New World Hydra, Breakout and Mutagen Cloud now show their real A side in Inspect instead of a second copy of the B side.
