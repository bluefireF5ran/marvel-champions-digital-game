## [v0.37.0] - 2026-10-26
### Added
* Every option of a choice is shown: one that can't be chosen is listed disabled with why (no valid target, a cost you can't pay, an unmet condition, or a card forbidding it), and when only one option is left a notice lists the others and why (#412)
### Changed
* The question an effect asks when it puts a "Play under any player's control" card into play names the card ("Who controls Boot Camp?") instead of a generic "Choose a player"
* Make the Call now always shows the allies it can take, each tagged with whose discard pile it is in, and you pick one before paying, even when only one ally fits; on a phone a long list of allies pages instead of running off the screen (#419).
### Fixed
* Cancelling a boost card's boost icons (Attacrobatics, Foiled, Preemptive Strike) no longer cancels the icons its own "Boost" ability gives it (ruling May 10, 2024, RRG p. 9)
* A "Play under any player's control" card put into play by an effect (Build Support finding Boot Camp) can now go under any player's control, chosen by the player putting it in (RRG pp. 32-33; ruling Nov 21, 2025)
* An enemy targeted by an "(attack)" ability is attacked even when it takes no damage (Tackle's stun, a confuse), so its Retaliate triggers (FFG ruling 197, May 30, 2024; RRG p. 10)
* The Infinity Stone that "I Am Inevitable" or Infinite Mischief applies as a boost card can be targeted by boost-card abilities such as Preemptive Strike (FFG ruling Sept 5, 2024 #8)
* A basic thwart whose side scheme leaves play before it removes threat now logs that the thwart ended (RRG p. 13)
* Other players can no longer trigger the abilities of a peril card in a player's play area, including an engaged minion or an attachment there (RRG p. 32)
* A card whose text puts another card into play under a stated player's control (Shadowland's and Mojo's recorded cards, the Mutant Genesis role upgrades, X-23's specializations) no longer asks who controls an any-player-control card (RRG p. 32)
* Guard now keeps an enemy off the choice of an (attack) ability that only stuns or confuses it (Unlikely Duo, Tackle, Flurry of Blades); RRG p. 10, ruling 197, owner ruling Q49
* Stakeout can be attached to a side scheme after it is played from your hand (#411)
* Work-Life Balance's refusal now says why it can't be used: no ally to remove, and a stalwart or already confused identity can't be confused (#412)
* Earth's Mightiest Heroes can no longer be played without a ready Avenger to exhaust, so an exhausted Hawkeye can't ready himself with it (#415).
* Make the Call asks which cards to pay with again when only one ally can be chosen and another card sits above it in the discard pile, instead of spending cards for you (#419, reported by @zackburnley).
* Heart of the Panther can be played with Black Panther (Shuri) and the T'Challa ally in play, as FFG has ruled (#425).
### Engine
* "In your play area" now includes cards attached to your identity or to your cards, so Stakeout no longer needs its own rule; Agent of Thanos and Channeling Trance count only Spell environments (#411)
### Content
* Music for Iron Man, Captain America and Spider-Woman in Civil War, and for Art Museum Heist, The Getaway, Protection Racket, The Raft Breakout and Stop the Presses! in Fear No Evil, each in the Extras jukebox under its title
### Docs
* Moved the Rules Reference copies to docs/rules/ and the post-RRG 1.7 rulings transcript to docs/rulings/, with every link, script and agent brief pointing at the new paths.
* Added a Codex review pass on open pull requests (scripts/codex-pr-review.mjs posts its findings as [Codex] inline comments), used only where Codex is installed.
