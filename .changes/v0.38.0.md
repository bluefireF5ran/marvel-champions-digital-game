## [v0.38.0] - 2026-10-27
### Added
* Full controller support: Xbox, PlayStation, Switch and Steam Deck controllers are detected and their own button glyphs show; left stick, held D-pad and right-stick scrolling work everywhere (#398).
* Settings ▸ Controls: a drawn controller for your layout, button labels override, Use Nintendo Switch layout, button and key hints on/off, and Keyboard hand (left or right).
* Controller and keyboard hints on the table and popups, beside the controls they press; they hide while the mouse moves and come back when it rests.
* Hold LT for Attack / Thwart / Recover / Change form (LB Undo, RB End turn) and RT for your cards' abilities; View reopens the villain phase recap; Menu pauses.
* Discord status in the desktop app: shows your game (hero, villain, campaign, round) on your Discord profile while Discord is open; turn it off in Settings.
* Adding ?fps=1 to the address shows a frame-rate readout, for checking a slow screen on a phone
* Settings ▸ Controls shows what the connected controller reports as you press it, to check a controller the game sees but doesn't respond to
* Couch mode (Settings ▸ Display) draws everything 125% to 300% bigger, for playing on a TV from across the room, in the browser and the desktop app; the setup screen's opening hand grows into the free space, and Sharper text follows couch mode
* Every screen's D-pad and keyboard focus reaches the music and report buttons; LB / RB switch tabs in Settings, Extras, Decks and Deck check; the right stick scrolls whatever is highlighted
* The music player answers the pad and keyboard: B / Esc closes it, A plays or pauses, LB / RB change the song, X toggles Shuffle; Pause has Music and Report a problem
* Take your seats: LB / RB pick the seat, X clears it, Y and B move between the filters and the hero list; the aspect (i) tips open only when pressed
* Campaign screens answer a controller and keyboard everywhere; the Market can be shopped by pad: Y inspects, A adds, B takes the cart back, X filters to what you can afford, Start finishes
* Controls: Show hints for (Auto, Controller or Keyboard) pins which hints the game draws; on a Steam Deck in Desktop Mode, where Steam turns the buttons into keys, pick Controller
* LT and RT page the focused hero or villain row, with glyphs by the arrows; Choose heroes and Play heroes show the Start glyph.
* Pay, Cancel and every mode-bar button can be reached and pressed with a pad (Start pays, B cancels); the villain phase starts on A; the defend Confirm shows its glyph; the music player opens from the board with a pad.
* Nevermind after End turn: take it back from the discard sheet, the villain phase's Start gate (B or Esc) or the next hero's turn, before anything is revealed.
* Controller: who attacks, target tiles and the like are picked outright with RT + A / X / Y / B (digits 1 to 9 on a keyboard); LB / RB under RT turn the page.
* Install for Steam scripts for Windows, macOS and Linux (each release ships its OS's): add the installed game to Steam as a non-Steam game with its icon and library art, and on Linux the AppImage's launch options, on any distro, the Steam Deck and Bazzite (#422)
* The hero panel shows each hero's current hand size, and the printed one when an effect changes it (Iron Man's Tech upgrades)
* Settings > Game > "Explain abilities not offered" (on by default) and the notice's own "Don't show again" button (X on a pad) turn off the not-offered notice; the log and Inspect still say why (#431).
* Community Discord link: the README, the issue chooser, release notes, and in the game on the Title screen footer and Report an issue (#438)
### Changed
* One-handed keyboard: WASD to move, Space select, Q back, E inspect, Z X C V basic actions, 1-4 abilities, Shift+Space end turn, Ctrl+Z undo (bare Z is now Attack); IJKL mirror for a mouse in the left hand.
* Start / Menu presses a screen's main forward button (Choose heroes, Play, Deal it out, Continue), and Start or View skips comics and stories
* Scenario select and Take your seats put the filter chips behind a Filters button (with a count) when a controller is in use or the game is under 1000 px wide; Y shows and hides them on a pad, and View inspects the focused tile.
* The Inspect sheet says B to close with a controller.
* Set the table shows B on Back and Start on Deal it out; holding Y (or E) enlarges the focused modular set's picture, and a tap of Y opens its cards.
* The game log panel's text is larger and its Hold/View chips sit under the title.
* Nevermind after End turn is strict: once the hand refill has drawn cards, the villain phase's Start gate no longer offers it.
* New game starts with no villain or hero picked, so every seat begins blank
* Controller hints draw real button icons on Xbox, PlayStation and Switch pads (CC BY 4.0 icon sets by Alric Monteiro, Antoine Plu and Gioele Casazza): face buttons, bumpers, triggers, D-pad, stick presses and the system buttons.
* Controller button icons are white with an ink outline on every background, so they stand out on dark screens as well as light ones.
### Fixed
* Mobile browsers with a bottom address bar (Edge on Android) no longer cut off the bottom row of the table and Setup.
* A controller that disconnects and reconnects works again at once, without reopening the screen
* On Linux (Bazzite, Steam Deck) the desktop app reads controllers itself instead of through the webview, which misread them: a stick that never let go, a dead D-pad, presses that only counted when held
* The Linux AppImage plays music and sounds: it now carries its own GStreamer media framework and codecs
* A long press no longer scrolls the list under it, and dragging a slider sideways no longer scrolls the page
* The Linux desktop app's audio no longer crackles: the webview's sound buffer is longer on PipeWire / PulseAudio
* The controller focus box hides when you move the mouse, follows a row as it scrolls, and comes back on the next pad or key press
* A controller can always leave a search, seed, deck-name or paste field, and leaving a setup screen ignores further presses
* The Trophy Room, Art & Credits, How to play, How to win, the aspect lessons, Report an issue and the sync and deck sheets can be fully operated by pad and keyboard
* Decks & Collection, Deck builder and Deck check: B backs out (closing an open box first), Y inspects cards, glyph hints never cover a control
* Glossary terms, guide option cards and a few other controls press only on a real tap, not when a swipe lifts over them
* Decks & Collection: a long press on a pool card opens Inspect while the finger is still down, and a held finger's wobble no longer scrolls the pool
* A stray A or Start can no longer answer a sync question that pops up mid-game, an open achievement list no longer passes its presses to the screen under it, and the Trophy Room and Credits keep Up and Down working after a scroll
* Rules reference, opening hand, pile view, saved games, save data, unlocks and round debrief work fully with a controller; the end-turn confirm can't be skipped by a double A
* A controller press on a still screen is no longer missed or delayed, and Pause on a phone puts Music, Report and Controls on one row so the Table and Game log keep their room.
* Linux app: music and sound effects now play natively through PulseAudio/PipeWire, which fixes the crackling and robotic sound on the Steam Deck.
* Hero tiles that were blank in the Linux app (Thor, Hulk, Star-Lord and six more): their pictures are re-encoded as plain WebP, and a tile whose picture fails to load falls back to the printed card.
* Linux app: card tiles and pictures no longer come out garbled or blank (Bazzite, Steam Deck); the card-face memory budget now evicts old faces, never one just baked.
* Settings' Resolution list takes the d-pad and A/B while open, instead of the controls behind it; back buttons with a pad glyph (Trophy Room and other top-bar screens) hold the glyph inside the button.
* Linux app: X/Y and the triggers read correctly on pads the bridge has no SDL mapping for, so the LT/RT hold layers, the board glyphs and Inspect work on the pad.
* Card faces: art evicted from the bake cache stays open briefly, so tiles baked during a mass redraw no longer end up blank.
* Controller: every control of the villain phase and its recap wears a glyph and answers A (Next card, Flip, Use, Start player phase); Let it resolve and Full log wear X; Nevermind on the discard sheet wears B; a greyed Confirm no longer takes A or Start.
* Review fixes: Linux native audio starts muted when Sound is saved off; a keyboard Inspect hold closes on its key's release even with Shift held; Discord presence comes back after Discord restarts while the game sits on one screen.
* The only-option notice works with a controller: OK wears A and Start, B closes it, and the press that closes it no longer opens Pause or presses the Board (#412).
* On Linux, the desktop app restarts the game page if WebKit's page process stops responding (seen after the screen sleeps or a TV turns off), instead of staying frozen
* When an interrupt or response matches what just happened but can't be used (no valid target, or a cost you can't pay, like Iron Lad with no other ready Avenger ally), the table now says why and the log records it (#431).
* With a controller, the D-pad reaches START VILLAIN PHASE as soon as the villain phase opens, instead of sometimes only the top bar's buttons, and Down from the top bar always gets back to the panel (#442).
* Retaliate damage is named in the game log and listed on the attack's row of the villain-phase recap, so Black Panther's Retaliate no longer looks like it did nothing (#444).
* The Extras rulebooks show each box's cover and comic pages instead of a "no text on this page" note, Civil War's included (#446, reported by @Perezh21).
