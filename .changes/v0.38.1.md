## [v0.38.1] - 2026-10-27
### Fixed
* Menu, settings and in-game text no longer looks slightly blurry on a monitor at 100% display scaling, such as 2560 x 1440 (#453).
* Clicking SKIP on a comic leaves it again, instead of turning a page (#452)
* Fear No Evil: fixing a deck from the Briefing keeps the scenario you picked (no second pick or opening comic), and Aftermath only scores the game dealt for the issue, never a stale one (#451)
* The campaign reward screen scrolls, so every hero can take a reward on a phone, and it shows a loading line instead of a black screen while the finished game is logged; confirming picks no longer replays the whole game each time (#448).
* Turning Couch mode off at a fixed resolution no longer shrinks the game into the bottom-left quarter of the screen.
