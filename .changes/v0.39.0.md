## [v0.39.0] - 2026-10-28
### Added
* Spanish card art: every release also ships Spanish installers and an APK (named -ES-) whose cards show the Spanish scans, with the English scan for any card that has no Spanish one, decided when the app is built (#337)
* Card art language on the web: a globe button in the top bar and a Settings choice switch the card pictures between English and Español; on the desktop and phone apps the globe explains which download has which language (#337)
* The bug report form asks which card language you played in (English or Spanish), and the in-game report fills it in (#337)
* Trophy Room: each category folds and opens with a tap (all start folded, and the room remembers which you opened), and a Filters bar sorts by category, completion, points or A–Z, either way, with Expand all / Collapse all (#479).
* Dark mode (#459): a moon/sun toggle at the top of every screen, and Settings > Display > Dark mode, draw the light screens and popups in warm charcoal with light text. Card art stays as printed.
* Contributors can add or replace card scans, music and art from a pull request by putting the files in incoming/ at their usual paths; after the merge a workflow packs them into the release bundles and opens the manifest pull request (#495)
* The v0.39.0 release report has its own page on the site, at /releases/v0.39.0/, so the release notes can link to it instead of carrying every image
### Changed
* The deck builder fills a wide window, adding card pool columns as it grows (#458)
* An optional response offered ahead of the forced ones says Not now instead of Decline, and that it is offered again once the forced responses resolve
* Card art, music and art moved out of git into versioned zip bundles on the content repo's releases: `pnpm assets:fetch` downloads them into a cache shared by every checkout, and `pnpm assets:pack` uploads a changed bundle (#495)
### Fixed
* The Power Of cards no longer double their resources when overpaying for the Ant-Man or Wasp allies (FFG ruling Sep 21, 2023, post-RRG 1.5 #87)
* Refilling to hand size draws one card at a time: a drawn card's "after this enters your hand" ability (Shapeshifter Surprise) or a drawn obligation entering play resolves before the next card is drawn, and Mysterio's encounter cards are still dealt after the whole draw (RRG p. 21; ruling Aug 14, 2025)
* A hero with two hero forms told to change to hero form while in one changes to the other (Ready for a Fight), and Bodyslide lets them (FFG rulings June 8, 2023 #22 and June 17, 2025; choosing among several other hero faces is an engine interpretation)
* Frenemies removes its two instances of threat simultaneously: a crisis icon side scheme it defeats still bars the main scheme from the second removal (FFG ruling December 8, 2023 - Ruling 127; RRG p. 61)
* Wolverine's Claws gives piercing only to the first attack the played event makes (Slice and Dice: the second attack does not pierce, unless a stun replaced the first), per FFG ruling 190, May 16, 2024
* I've Been Waiting For This! as a boost: the villain's scheme now waits until the boosted attack and its responses finish (RRG p. 6, ruling 214, Jun 13, 2024)
* Gamma Blast and Photon Speed resolve their whole first sentence before the energy form response, so a tough enemy blocks the 7 damage of Gamma Blast (FFG ruling Dec 8, 2023 #126 on Gamma Blast, applied to Photon Speed by analogy; RRG p. 4)
* Nebula's precon cards no longer show a faint watermark: clean scans for eleven of its cards, from Cutthroat Ambition to Brains Over Brawn
* An enemy activation started while another enemy activation resolves now waits for it to finish, whatever started it (a response or a forced response as well as a Boost ability), and keeps its player and event scope; a replacement activation still resolves at once (RRG 1.8 p. 6, ruling 214)
* Upgraded Drones and any other card attached to an environment now shows on the environment's tile, so its ability (Upgraded Drones' spend-to-discard) can be used (#462)
* The title menu scrolls on a window too short for it, such as a phone held landscape
* The rest of Nebula's pack, thirteen cards from Gamora to Defensive Training, no longer shows a faint watermark, and Nebula's hero and alter-ego sides use the owner's own scans
* The desktop card hover always enlarges the card: at least 1.5x its size on screen, so large cards in the deck builder on a wide window come up bigger (#470).
* Android and Linux preview builds of a merged pull request no longer linger on the Releases page (#472)
* The Windows Install for Steam script now ships as a .cmd you double-click, so it runs after downloading with no execution policy change (#474)
* A text field (the Seats search bar) stays in place under a card hover popover, with the card drawn over it, instead of blinking out (#470).
* The villain phase's "You drew N cards" line and the New tags in your hand count every card of the end-of-turn draw, also when it stopped for a drawn card's ability
* A response sheet in a window shared by several triggering conditions says which one the offered responses answer (a form change's Response after Gamma Blast is headed by the form change, not the attack)
* The game log names the hero face an identity with several changed to ("You changed to Giant Wasp.")
* A draw up to hand size nested in another paused for the same player no longer deals the outer refill's encounter cards (Mysterio) before that refill finishes
* Wolverine's Claws: when a played event's first attack is cancelled, its next attack is the first made and gains piercing (FFG ruling 190; RRG 1.8 Cancel p. 11)
* A sideways flick of a hero or scenario shelf now keeps coasting after you lift your finger, as the vertical scroll already did (#478).
* Android's back gesture, a browser's Back and a mouse's back button now step back a screen (closing what is open, or opening Pause in a game) instead of closing the app; on Title they leave as before (#480).
* Spider-Man's and Captain Marvel's core decks and nemesis sets use the owner's own straight, clean scans, so Captain Marvel's crooked card with a white tab is gone (#485)
* Black Panther's core deck and nemesis set, every core aspect and basic card, and the Rise of Red Skull aspect cards use the owner's own scans
* The release workflow clones blobless and times out after 30 minutes, so a slow clone no longer holds every later release run in the queue.
* A cloud sync test no longer fails on a slow CI runner: Continue's timeout is fired by hand once the conflict question is up.
* The Rise of Red Skull: after beating Zola, each hero is offered only their own Basic upgrade to flip to its Improved side, instead of every Condition card in the first hero's deck (#496).
* A controller can now reach the villain, schemes, minions and attachments with LB/RB and the D-pad, and Y (or A) opens them in Inspect (#506)
### Engine
* A sentence's responses open as one window after it resolves, where players order responses to different triggering conditions freely and forced responses come before optional ones only for the same condition (RRG 1.8 p. 45, p. 38, p. 5, p. 20)
* A response that cannot be used is not reported as not offered in the middle of a sentence that holds its responses, as during a reveal
* An ability that starts an enemy activation while another one resolves now waits with it: its later instructions read what that activation did, and resume as soon as it finishes (RRG 1.8 p. 6)
* When one effect makes several enemies activate, the villains activate before the minions, and the first player orders each group of two or more (RRG 1.8 p. 6)
