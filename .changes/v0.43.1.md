## [v0.43.1] - 2026-11-03
### Fixed
* Wiccan's response no longer asks for an enemy when the discarded card has no boost icons, and the log now says how many boost icons a card effect counted and always names an ally's consequential damage (#627).
* Rotating from portrait to landscape no longer leaves the game short with a black band at the bottom, and the faded campaign box art now runs behind the campaign pages: the run, issues, dossier, briefing, market and more (#629)
* Editing a deck before a campaign scenario now has the deck builder's From your games stats and Review, scrolling the card pool is much smoother on tablets, tablets fold the filters behind a Filters chip for a taller pool, and the Saga scrolls its volumes, stacks the selected campaign over them on phones and upright tablets, and has a list or box art grid to choose from there (#631).
* Slingshot's Action can be used from your hand: a tap on her asks Play it or her Action (or starts the Action when she cannot be played), Inspect offers both, and she can no longer pay for her own Action (#634).
* Minions stay readable beside an environment on a phone, and a minion's Inspect sheet on a phone shows its HP (#635).
