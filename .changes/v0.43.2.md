## [v0.43.2] - 2026-11-05
### Fixed
* The Wrecking Crew: each villain's encounter deck no longer holds a second copy of his signature side scheme, which starts in play (15 cards per deck, as the insert says).
* Sweeping Swoop no longer stuns an alter-ego, and Under Attack and Electric Whip Attack no longer offer an alter-ego player the option to damage their hero (RRG 1.8 "Form, Change Form", p. 21).
* Crushing Blow can be played again when the hand holds a [physical] card or a wild resource alongside cards of other types; it no longer reads as unaffordable (#648).
### Engine
* A main scheme's "after placing threat here" no longer answers the threat that completed the stage before it: Assault on NORAD no longer asks each player for 2 threat or a Drone on the round The Crimson Cowl is completed.
