# Shadowland art

Owner-kept pictures, saved 2026-10-20, live 2026-10-22. Artwork (c) Marvel; Marvel Champions is Fantasy Flight Games'.
The Shadowland insert (MC65) has no comic page, so these tell the box.

- `cover.png`: the Saga tile and Cover screen; a copy of `artboards/shadowland-daredevil.png` (Daredevil in black amid
  Hand ninjas).
- `artboards/` (700x500): five pieces of Marvel Champions: Shadowland (MC65) promotional art,
  from the bottom of Hall of Heroes' Shadowland page (https://hallofheroeslcg.com/shadowland/); the artist is not
  recorded. They carry no on-screen credit, like every other box's FFG art. Issue openers:
  `shadowland-daredevil` and `hand-ninja` (Shadows in the Night), `dojo` and `daredevil-flame` (Shadow Labyrinth),
  `throne` (Heart of Shadow).
- `pages/` (lettered, so the reader adds no balloons): six pages of Marvel's _Shadowland_ event (2010; written by Andy
  Diggle, pencils by Billy Tan), found online and shared by the owner, in the order shared (`01` to `06`). The exact
  issue of each page is not confirmed. They are read as the aftermath beats between issues, and every screen that
  shows one prints the series credit (the panels print none). `06-end-your-suffering.jpg` was downscaled from 2016x3200 to 1260x2000 (JPEG
  q85) to keep the bundle small.
- All of it is also on Extras > Artwork, earned (`progression/shadowland-extras.ts`).
