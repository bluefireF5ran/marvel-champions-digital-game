# Wave 10, question 30: Quickstrike after When Revealed. Impact list

Survey only. Nothing in the engine, scripts, tests or data was changed. Written 2026-10-12, before the change.

**Decision (owner, 2026-10-12, question 30 = B).** For a minion being **revealed**, its When Revealed abilities
resolve first, then Quickstrike. This supersedes the February 28, 2026 ruling (Hellcat) and the owner's wave 6 answer
(question 2), which the engine follows today. A minion put into play without being revealed still triggers Quickstrike
on engagement and has no When Revealed.

How things were checked: RRG pages were rendered from `mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf` and read; engine code was
read, not run; card lists come from a text scan of every `packages/content/src/data/*/cards.ts` (59 packs, 3729 card
records). No test was run. Where a claim rests on reading code only, it says so.

### 1.2 The new order within one reveal

| #   | Step                                 | What happens                                                                                                                                                          | Change              |
| --- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| 1   | Faceup (RRG step 1)                  | Card turned faceup; "when revealed" interrupts and cancels.                                                                                                           | none                |
| 2   | Enters play and engages (RRG step 2) | Minion enters the revealing player's play area engaged with them. Interrupts to entering and to the engagement resolve here. Toughness is placed here (open point 2). | none                |
| 2b  | Unique check                         | A matching unique card turns the minion away; nothing below resolves.                                                                                                 | none                |
| 3   | When Revealed (RRG step 3)           | Incite, then each When Revealed ability on the card.                                                                                                                  | now before keywords |
| 4   | Discard (RRG step 4)                 | Treachery only.                                                                                                                                                       | none                |
| 5   | Reveal finished: engagement keywords | **Quickstrike**, then **Teamwork**, each read as it resolves.                                                                                                         | **moved here**      |
| 6   | Reveal finished: responses           | Forced, then optional responses to the card being revealed, to the minion engaging, to a treachery resolving.                                                         | none (already here) |
| 7   | Surge                                | The surge deal, last.                                                                                                                                                 | none                |

**Superseded for row 7** (RRG v1.8 p.42; owner decision 2026-10-16 (audit finding 16)): surge is a When Revealed
ability, so its card is dealt in row 3, after the incite and the printed When Revealed abilities, before the keywords
and every response; the dealt card is still revealed later, in the player's queue (`resolve/reveal.ts`, stage `surge`).

"The reveal process finishes" means steps 1 to 4 of p. 38 are done for that one card. Consequences:

| Case                                                               | Under the new order                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Interrupts to the engagement ("when a minion engages you")         | Unchanged, step 2. Gunslinger 39069 ("before resolving quickstrike") still precedes both.                                                                                                                            |
| Responses to the engagement ("after you engage a minion")          | Unchanged in position (step 6), still after Quickstrike. They now also follow the When Revealed in every case, which they already did.                                                                               |
| Responses to the card entering play ("after a minion enters play") | Should be step 6 by p. 38 and FAQ #10. Today they resolve in step 2 (open point 1).                                                                                                                                  |
| Surge                                                              | Unchanged: the deal is last, and the dealt card waits facedown.                                                                                                                                                      |
| Minion dealt as a boost card                                       | Not revealed and not in play: no change. A boost ability that says "put this minion into play" is a put into play (section 3.4). A boost ability that says "reveal" it (Cloak 56194, Dagger 56195) is a full reveal. |
| "Reveal the top card", "discard until a minion, reveal it"         | Same procedure (p. 38), so the same new order. A reveal nested inside another card's When Revealed finishes, keywords and responses included, before the outer card's step 3 goes on.                                |
| "Find and reveal" a minion already in play                         | p. 19: it engages (unless already engaged with that player), does not enter play, then its When Revealed, then Quickstrike. No Teamwork, as today.                                                                   |
| Reveal with all effects cancelled                                  | Minion is discarded; no Quickstrike, as today.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Only the When Revealed cancelled                                   | Quickstrike still resolves: it is not a When Revealed ability.                                                                                                                                                       |
| Put into play, engage, rotate engagement, cost-arrow entry         | No reveal: Quickstrike on engagement, as today (section 3.4).                                                                                                                                                        |
