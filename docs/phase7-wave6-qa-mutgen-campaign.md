## 5. Not testable here, and why

- **Deck and aspect changes between scenarios (row 3)**: the campaign log stores a seat's deck, and swapping it is the
  engine runner's and the client's job (no MC32 instruction exists for it), so the definition has nothing to assert.
- **A real win through all five scenarios**: the greedy headless driver cannot win any of these games (qa's finding), so
  the full walk keeps substituted outcomes; row 1's confidence is for the definition and the log, not for the winnability
  of the scenarios.
- **The campaign log sheet's printed layout (p. 24)**: a paper artifact; its fields are all declared in the definition
  and checked by row 8's test.
