## Sources, in order of authority

1. The owner's answers, `docs/phase7-wave8.md` §4.1 rows 1 to 72 (binding where they speak).
2. RRG 1.8 (`mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf`), cited "RRG p. N". Page numbers are the PDF's printed pages; entries are named as the RRG's index names them.

Community pages are not used. The Wrecking Crew insert is not in the repo (see "Could not verify").

## How to read the verdicts

- **Supported**: an official source (RRG, FFG ruling, rulebook, printed card text) says so; it is named.
- **Owner decision (Qn)**: settled by the owner's answer, with no official source. This is not the same as Supported and is never described as an FFG rule.
- **Consistent**: no source speaks to this case; the reading follows from a named general rule.
- **Interpretation**: reasonable, but another reading is equally open (one line).
- **Contradicts**: a source or owner answer says otherwise; what the game should do is given, and a test pins it.

Test names are file names. Engine tests are in `packages/engine/src/`; card-side tests are in `packages/cards/src/`. "Today" means what the test proves the game does now. Where no test pins the behavior, the entry says "no test" and the reading comes from the code.

**Totals (36 entries).** Supported 8, Owner decision 5, Consistent 12, Interpretation 10, Contradicts 1.

**Work in progress by others.** The engine agents are changing rewards, Desperate Measures, the expert heal, Attacrobatics, cancelled attacks and main scheme counters. None of the 36 entries below depends on those. Entry 9 (Panicked Refugees) cites a test in `wave8/aoa/campaign/`, a folder they are editing; the behavior judged is the draw rule, not campaign text. ## For the owner to rule on

Ranked by gameplay impact, most first. The recommended option is first in each.

1. **Quickstrike order (entry 20).** RRG 1.8 p. 36 says quickstrike resolves after When Revealed; the February 28, 2026 ruling says before. This touches every minion reveal in the game (the Hellcat case). A: keep the ruling (as built). B: follow the RRG text. Recommend A, because the ruling answers the exact question; but ask FFG or confirm that RRG 1.8's unchanged bullet is a leftover.
2. **Panicked Refugees (entry 9).** A: keep the RRG p. 30 reading (a drawn obligation goes to the play area), where the card's reveal-and-draw does nothing on an ordinary draw. B: treat a draw of this card as entering the hand, so it is revealed and replaced by a draw. Recommend B, because as built the card's text almost never applies; the card is in every campaign deck.
3. **Pestilence blanks which face (entry 4).** A: the card, so both faces (as built). B: only the face showing when she attacks. Recommend A, because the printed text says "your identity's text box" and one card is one object.
4. **Overpaying a chosen-size cost (entry 27).** A: refuse (as built). B: allow it, as RRG p. 13 allows any cost, and let Q34 pick the paid set. Recommend B, because the stricter rule makes some legal-feeling payments impossible (Husk with two cards).
5. **Overpaid payment ties (entry 25).** A: ask the player which resources count as paid. B: fixed order physical, mental, energy, wild (as built). Recommend A when the tie changes which line of a card fires (Multitalented), B otherwise; either way, say so on the payment prompt.
6. **Grounded and an encounter card's Action (entry 29).** A: she pays the 2 resources (the change is to hero form during her turn). B: free (as built). Recommend A, because the printed condition is met literally; the owner's Q37 only freed changes outside her turn.
7. **Breakout and nemesis sets (entry 24).** A: follow the January 17, 2026 ruling and set nemesis sets aside in Breakout. B: follow the insert as the spec reads it (as built). Recommend A once the insert is read by the owner; the ruling is the only text in the repo that speaks to Face the Past there.
8. **Cell Phone and a divided power (entry 8).** A: Wasp may divide it. B: never divided (as built). Recommend A, because her divided power is still her basic attack (FAQ p. 61), but it is rare.
9. **Keep Up the Pressure (entry 15).** A: ATTACK-trait events only (as built). B: any (attack)-labeled event. Recommend A.
10. **Excess damage and the floor (entry 34).** A: leave as built. B: floor-aware. Recommend A until a card puts the floor on an ally, minion or identity.

One **Contradicts** is not a question: entry 2 (Generation X on a divided thwart) is fixed to the card text when an engine agent is free. It cannot show in play until an X-Men character can divide a basic thwart.
