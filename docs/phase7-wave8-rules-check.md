# Wave 8 rules check: attack-window timing, mission-area effects, campaign reward eligibility

Checked 2026-10-08 on `feature/wave-8`, at the owner's request (Q56 note): the "built this way" readings in PR 103 are not confirmed in bulk. This page checks every built behavior in three areas against the primary sources and gives a verdict for each. Nothing was changed; the three **Contradicts** are pinned by tests.

## Sources, in order of authority

1. The owner's own answers, `docs/phase7-wave8.md` §4.1 rows Q1 to Q56 and the two follow-ups (binding where they speak).
2. RRG 1.8 (`mc_rulesreference_v18_compressed.pdf`), cited by entry and page. Pages come from the RRG's own index.
3. FFG rulings since RRG 1.7 (`marvel-champions-rulings-post-rrg-1-7.md`), cited by date heading. No ruling and the RRG disagree in the points below.
4. The Age of Apocalypse rulebook, cited "MC45 p. N", and the printed card text in `packages/content/src/data/<pack>/cards.ts`. The eight page images in `art/campaigns/aoa/rulebook/` are the box's comic pages, not its rules pages (see "Could not verify"), so the rules pages were read from the repo's transcription `docs/campaign-modes/markdown/mc45_age_of_apocalypse.md`. The log sheet (p. 24) was read as an image from `docs/campaign-modes/log-sheets/mc45_age_of_apocalypse_campaign_log.pdf`.

## How to read the verdicts

- **Supported**: a source says so directly (it is named).
- **Consistent**: no source speaks to this case; the reading follows from a general rule (named).
- **Interpretation**: reasonable, but another reading is equally open (given in one line).
- **Contradicts**: a source or an owner answer says otherwise; what the game should do is given, and a test pins it.

Test names are file names. Engine tests are in `packages/engine/src/`. Card-side tests are in `packages/cards/src/`: `wave8/aoa/campaign/` holds the `mission-*.test.ts` files, `campaigns/aoa.test.ts` the reward tests, and `qa-2026-10-07-attack-labeled.test.ts`, `wave5/nova/events.test.ts` and `wave8/effect-declared-defense.test.ts` sit directly under it. "Today" means what the test proves the game does now.

**Totals.** Attack window: 5 Supported, 4 Consistent, 2 Interpretation, 1 Contradicts. Mission area: 10 Supported, 5 Consistent, 2 Interpretation, 2 Contradicts. Campaign rewards: 9 Supported, 2 Consistent, 6 Interpretation, 0 Contradicts.

## For the owner to rule on

Ranked by effect on play. The first option of each is my recommendation.

1. **Reward and deck size (C1).** What does "does not count against your minimum deck size" mean for a reward ally, upgrade or support?
   - **B (recommended): print-literal.** The reward is not counted toward the 40 minimum but counts toward the 50 maximum: 40 ordinary cards plus the reward. It is the engine's existing `maximumOnly` mode and matches Q25's own table wording.
   - A: as built. The reward counts as one of the 40 (39 plus the reward is legal), per the Q25 follow-up.
   - C: fully exempt from both limits, as MC10 p. 3 words it.
2. **When an "(attack)" ability's attack begins (A1, Contradicts RRG p. 26).**
   - **A (recommended): as the ability begins resolving**, before any earlier instruction, so "when you attack" interrupts see the attack first.
   - B: as built, at the first damage instruction with an attackable enemy.
3. **Mikhail Rasputin's Mission Response (M8, Contradicts RRG p. 20).**
   - **A (recommended): choose one ally, deal it N damage as one instance.**
   - B: as built, one choice per [energy] icon.
4. **Allies an effect plays, with a mission in play (M16, Contradicts MC45 p. 5).**
   - **A (recommended): offer the player the mission area as for a played card.** The engine team already has this queued.
