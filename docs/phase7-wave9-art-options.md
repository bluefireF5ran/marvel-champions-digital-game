# Wave 9 art options (Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. and Trickster Takeover)

Candidates for the fifteen open art slots, gathered 2026-10-09 for the owner to choose from. Nothing has been
downloaded into the repo. Every option is a file on Marvel Database (marvel.fandom.com); sizes and page links come
from the wiki's own API (`api.php`, `prop=imageinfo|info`). The wiki's HTML pages answered a scripted fetch with
HTTP 403, so the file pages themselves were not opened: candidates were found through the API's category, prefix and
search listings, and each link below is the canonical URL the API returned for a file it confirmed exists.

How each image was looked at: every option was seen as a 260 px thumbnail on a contact sheet; the top picks and most
runners-up were then looked at again at 500 px wide. Nothing was viewed at full size, so a small signature, corner
mark or fine print may have been missed. Artist names are the wiki's own credit tags on the file, not checked against
the comic, and are given as "wiki credits". Only Marvel Database was searched: larger or cleaner copies may exist
elsewhere. All are portrait unless stated. Open a page link to see the picture.

Where the picked file goes:

- Slots 1 and 2: `art/heroes/_pending/50001a-maria-hill/hero.<ext>` and
  `art/heroes/_pending/50034a-nick-fury/hero.<ext>`. Existing hero pictures are a mix (Black Widow is a 744×1040
  portrait, Winter Soldier a 700×620 promo crop), mostly a single figure from the knees or waist up.
- Slots 3 to 9: `art/scenarios/_pending/<id>/villain.<ext>` (ids `black-widow`, `batroc`, `modok`, `thunderbolts`,
  `baron-zemo`, `enchantress`, `loki-god-of-lies`), with optional `villain-wins`, `villain-loses` and `intro`.
- Slot 10: `art/campaigns/aos/cover.<ext>`. Existing covers are 1920×1920 (gmw), 1600×1200 (mts) or portrait
  (aoa 994×1528, mojo 792×1200); every candidate here is portrait.
- Slots 11 to 15: `art/teamups/<pair>/splash.<ext>` (portrait, like gamora-nebula's 736×1137) and `badge.<ext>`
  (roughly square close-up of both faces, about 490×400 to 580×520, cropped from a larger picture).

## 6 (second round). The Thunderbolts

The owner rejected 6A to 6D because none shows the whole team clearly. Every option here was looked at as a 420 px
thumbnail, not at full size; sizes and links were resolved through the wiki's API. Preview sheet:
`docs/design-renders/wave9-art-options/sheet-thunderbolts.jpg`. The original six are Citizen V, Meteorite (Moonstone),
MACH-1, Songbird, Atlas and Techno. In the game's Thunderbolts set (cards 50129 to 50160) Citizen V is the villain
and the minions are Moonstone, Songbird, MACH-IV, Jolt, Black Widow and Radioactive Man; Atlas and Techno have no
card. No clean, textless, large picture of all six turned up: the clean ones are small and the large ones carry
lettering.
