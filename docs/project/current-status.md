# Current status

[README](../../README.md) · [Current status](current-status.md) · [Architecture](architecture.md) · [Downloads](downloads.md) · [Contributing](../../CONTRIBUTING.md)

The game is playable end to end, solo or with 1–4 heroes played multi-handed on one device, across **every released
Marvel Champions product**: the Core Set through cycle 11, Fear No Evil, and the Shadowland scenario pack (released
October 16, 2026). That is 64 packs, 69 heroes, 68 scenarios and 331 encounter sets.

What you can play:

- **Every hero, scenario and encounter set from the Core Set through Shadowland**: the hero packs, campaign boxes and
  scenario packs of The Rise of Red Skull, The Galaxy's Most Wanted, The Mad Titan's Shadow, Sinister Motives, Mutant
  Genesis, NeXt Evolution, Age of Apocalypse, Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D., Civil War (with Synthezoid Smackdown), and Fear
  No Evil (with Jessica Jones, Luke Cage and Shadowland), each card scripted against its printed text and errata and
  covered by rules tests.
- **Campaign mode for all eleven campaigns**: The Rise of Red Skull, Galaxy's Most Wanted, The Mad Titan's Shadow,
  Sinister Motives, Mutant Genesis, MojoMania, NeXt Evolution, Age of Apocalypse, Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D., Fear No Evil
  and Shadowland, with the campaign log carried between scenarios, expert campaign rules, story beats and each box's
  own between-game choices.
- **Civil War's custom scenario builder**: pick a leader, two main scheme stages and three or four modular sets, or let
  the game deal a random (or random and blind) scenario.
- **Decks**: every hero's starter deck, decks built in the in-app deck builder, and decks imported from MarvelCDB, all
  checked against the Rules Reference's deckbuilding rules. Community decklists from MarvelCDB are on the Decks screen.
- **Standard and expert difficulty**, a **Guided mode** with Try-it lessons for new players, **Undo** for your own
  moves this turn, and **local save and resume**.
- **Your saves on every device**: send saves to another phone, tablet or computer by code or QR
  ([#340](https://github.com/quincarter/marvel-champions-digital-game/pull/340)), export them from the desktop app ([#343](https://github.com/quincarter/marvel-champions-digital-game/pull/343)), and keep saved games, decks, campaigns and
  unlocks in sync across devices with no login ([#354](https://github.com/quincarter/marvel-champions-digital-game/pull/354)).
- **Desktop, tablet, phone, keyboard and gamepad**: one client that runs in the browser and ships as desktop and Android
  apps, with the card hover popover on desktop and a thumb-reach layout on phones.

The latest release is **v0.33.0** (October 21, 2026); its notes are in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md). Shadowland
and the cross-device saves arrive in the next release.

Where each phase stands, and what comes next, is in the [roadmap](../../README.md#roadmap).
