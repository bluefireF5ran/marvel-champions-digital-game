Hall of Heroes is the transcriber, not the authority. The newer rulings (December 2025 onward, post-RRG 1.7 and 1.8) are in [`marvel-champions-rulings-post-rrg-1-7.md`](../../marvel-champions-rulings-post-rrg-1-7.md) at the repo root; check them and RRG v1.8 before relying on anything here.

The card and keyword lists below are matched mechanically against the repo's card data and a fixed keyword list, so they are a search aid, not a complete list.
