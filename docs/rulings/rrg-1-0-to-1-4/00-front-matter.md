## [Some of these rulings might be outdated – please refer to this page that covers rulings after RRG 1.5’s May 18, 2023 release](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-5/)

Here is a quick collection of rulings released by official FFG channels. Rules will be compiled for the lifetime of the game, so make sure you bookmark the page and come back to it periodically if you have a question. Expansions will be listed in chronological order with a heading for easy search capabilities.

[Here are links](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/products/marvel-champions-the-card-game/) to the most recently updated Rules Reference Guide (FAQ) and the Learn to Play booklets.

If you have a question for FFG, you can submit it [on the Rules Question Form](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/contact/rules/).

[Here is an unofficial restricted/taboo list](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/marvel-champions-lcg-unofficial-taboo-list/) designed by community members and Hall of Heroes.

You can find [a timing chart here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/1/d/1ExlEf5bcbBE_j5ASv_ddSZkAWqaORHkJuG0UFQNdUJc/htmlview#) from community member Apotropaic, as well as [another timing chart here](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/12yLiIhSRL-bs0WAuzi6Q7AxkXZMvIlEpB2xh8cEn39w/edit#slide=id.p).
