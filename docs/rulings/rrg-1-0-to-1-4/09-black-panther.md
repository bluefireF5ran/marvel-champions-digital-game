## Black Panther

> If an enemy initiates an attack against [Black Panther](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/tc0b.jpg?strip=info&w=1062) but another hero or an ally defends against that attack, will Black Panther’s retaliate ability trigger?
>
> [Second], if Black Panther defends against an attack, will his retaliate ability trigger?

No. Black Panther himself must be attacked for retaliate to trigger.

[Second], yes. As long as Black Panther himself is attacked, retaliate will trigger.

[-Official FAQ](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/4e/cd/4ecda1b3-22b0-4eb8-a2e3-7236afd92aa3/mc_rulesreference_v11.pdf) (1.1)

> Can [Ancestral Knowledge](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/tc2.jpg?strip=info&w=1080) shuffle different versions of [Wakanda Forever](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/tc4.jpg?strip=info&w=1062) into Black Panther’s deck?

No. Cards with the same title are considered to be the same card for the purpose of card abilities.

[-Official FAQ](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/4e/cd/4ecda1b3-22b0-4eb8-a2e3-7236afd92aa3/mc_rulesreference_v11.pdf) (1.1)

> Does moving damage with [Vibranium Suit](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/tc15.jpg?w=1500&h=) discard tough status cards from the target enemy?

Yes. If damage is moved to a character, the moved damage is considered to be dealt to that character.

[-Official FAQ](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/4e/cd/4ecda1b3-22b0-4eb8-a2e3-7236afd92aa3/mc_rulesreference_v11.pdf) (1.1)
