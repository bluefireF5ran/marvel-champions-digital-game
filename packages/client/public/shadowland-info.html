Where did the Shadowland content go?

Some Shadowland cards have been taken out of the game for now. We can't share why, but we want them back as soon as we can, and you can help.

TemporaryScans wantedAny number of cards helps

We're looking for fresh card scans

To bring the missing cards back we need new scans of the Shadowland box. If you own it and can photograph or scan the cards, we'd love your help. Even a handful of cards gets us closer.

  1. Open an issue on GitHub

    Open a new issue and tell us which Shadowland cards you can scan.

  2. We'll reply there

    We'll answer on the issue with how to send the images, so nothing is done twice.

What makes a good scan

  • Shot straight on, not at an angle.
  • All the card text is sharp and readable.
  • About 900px wide or larger.
  • Both sides of any double-sided card.

In the meantime: everything else in the game works as before. The missing cards come back in an update once we have scans for them.
